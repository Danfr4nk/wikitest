## What survives and what doesn't

The page is a faithful summary of a session the repository cannot audit,
and the old wiki itself later corrected the session's central fact. The
correct posture is therefore: the gemini-13 chain is **internally
documented but externally mislabeled** — the wrong-title-for-five-years
reading comes from the prior wiki, not from this ingest, and the page's
tenancy dates and death-date certainty are the kinds of detail that drifted
between the session transcript and the page. The genuinely improbable
shape (childhood-neighbour commercial → novelist → mother's rental → Dan's
address) does not depend on which novel it is; that is why the episode
survives its own correction.
