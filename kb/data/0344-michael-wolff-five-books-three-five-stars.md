author page's "5 books, 3 rated 5 stars", the shelf's top-authors count of
5, and the shelf's 5-star table capping at Wolff's 3. Because the shelf
tables are machine-derived from the master CSV rather than hand-written,

## What it does not confirm

The primary CSV is unheld; the shelf tables inherit the same source the
author page used, so this is corroboration across pages, not across
sources. The "almost certainly the highest of any non-fiction author
overall" hedge stands because the 5-star table only enumerates authors with
multiple 5-stars — nobody has *more* than 3, which is all the claim needs.
The ethical/methodological framing of Wolff (unguarded access,
participant-observer, the forensic-method link) is the prior wiki's own
reading of the books and the taste data; it was not checked against
publication metadata and is filed as attributed-to-the-page.
