## The six indicators the page lists

1. **Boundary persistence:** Annie remains blocked rather than the block
   becoming a dramatic announcement.
2. **Low-amplitude continuity:** Ally remains present without the relationship
   needing a crisis to sustain contact.
3. **Skepticism tolerance:** Ally's distrust does not automatically trigger
   withdrawal, retaliation, or an enormous persuasion campaign.
4. **Behavior over argument:** Dan actually performs a concrete correction
   Ally asks for (texting his father).
5. **Ordinary-life bandwidth:** the conversation moves from existential
   material to hockey, work, food, television, family, and jokes without every
   transition becoming emotionally loaded.
6. **No immediate verdict:** neither person has to declare everything fixed.

The page says any one would be weak evidence; their simultaneous appearance
is what merits the second look.

## What this page is — and is not — as evidence

This is operator analysis written 2026-08-26, two weeks before ingest. It is
not a primary record of the conversation it describes (that transcript is not
in this repository). The one checkable behavioral claim it carries — Annie
blocked, several days without scheming — is asserted by Dan to the operator,
not verified against a held source. The rest is an interpretation, explicitly
hedged (\"the transcript does not establish that this transition is
complete\"), and its strongest feature is epistemic hygiene: it refuses its
own premature diagnosis and names the falsification condition — boring,
ordinary, unobserved consistency over time. The datum is the reading, not the
facts it reads.
