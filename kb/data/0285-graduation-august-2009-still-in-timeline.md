## What this is

A generated page propagating a claim its own corpus has already killed. The
August 2009 graduation survives in three layers: the source page
(`full-sail-2008-2010` · The degree), the old wiki's generated timeline, and
the new master timeline. dat:0058's operator correction (September 2009, with
the audit/lab/certification account reconciling the November–December 2009
coursework) exists only in the kb, not on the source page — so every
regeneration of the timeline will keep printing August.

## What has to happen

The fix belongs on the source page (`wiki/timeline/periods/full-sail-2008-2010`
· The degree), not on the timeline: per the page's own header, *"To correct an
entry, fix the page it came from; the fix appears here on the next run."* The
open-questions contradiction entry should then be retired by the same edit.
Until then the timeline presents a Tier-1 'fact' adjacent to its own refutation
— the exact failure class [`dat:0052`](0052-old-wiki-instrument-layer-three-rules.md)
and [`dat:0053`](0053-old-wiki-instruments-corrected-each-other.md) describe,
except here the instruments did *not* correct each other.

## Still open

The September 2009 correction is operator testimony (attributed), not a
transcript or diploma. A Full Sail transcript would move it from testimony to
first-party record — noted in dat:0058 and repeated here because the timeline
entry is the highest-visibility place the stale claim still stands.
