## What the check confirms

This is one of the strongest artifact-level replications in the pass. The
page's corroboration table — the four structures, the 26-row dyadic thread,
the silence window, the "no eviction to Annie" prediction — reproduces from
an independent export with every timestamp and every quoted line intact. The
"Do it about 9" / "Thanks for the help" pair, 28 minutes apart, is exactly
where the page says it is, and it reads exactly as strangely as the page
says it does.

## What it does not confirm

The decode. The arrangement (Dan telephoning Paci in January 2025 with the
TBI story, Paci agreeing to perform the eviction, the parents' verification
calls) lives in a single operator capture this repository does not hold. The
held corpus corroborates the *structures the decode predicts* — the silence,
the thank-you, the one-sided "eviction" vocabulary, the July follow-ups
showing no awareness — which is the most the message record can do for a
claim about telephone calls. The page's own honesty about this ("Nothing in
Paci's own voice confirms the arrangement") is carried forward intact.
