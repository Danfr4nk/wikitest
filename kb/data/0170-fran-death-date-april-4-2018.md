## What the check confirms

The death date is now over-determined: Marla's last \"made it thru the
nite\" on the morning of April 4, Davey's condolence the next day, the
page's April 1/4 resolution note, and the old wiki's agreement. The
corpus-verified 07:23 EST timestamp on Marla's message is the hard anchor —
it is the kind of message that only gets sent while someone is still alive
the morning after, and it stops after April 4.

## What it does not confirm

The snow (`1080p.mov`), the Lucie Dobbin message text, and the memorial
invitation are the page's claims from uploads/captures not held in this
repository. The emotional-weight claims (closure, high-water mark of the
Annie decade) are Dan's contemporaneous words and the page's synthesis,
not independently verifiable facts.
