## What this corrects

The dossier baseline on `wiki/timeline/periods/2015-2016-annie-relationship-start`
placed \"YOU ARE MY EVERYTHING\" on \"day two\"/\"day three\" and quoted
*\"I met someone that instantly changed my life\"* without an address. The
read dates the first to 03:24 EST on Nov 29 — roughly ninety minutes after
the golf-course meeting — and addresses the second to Annie at 03:28 the
same night. Escalation speed in this relationship is therefore measurable in
hours from first in-person contact, which is the hard date any later claim
about the relationship's tempo has to survive.
