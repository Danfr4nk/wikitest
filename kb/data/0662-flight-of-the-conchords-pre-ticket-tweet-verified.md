## What this is evidence for

The page's interpretive move — using the timestamp to sequence interest
before purchase — is fully supported: the timestamp is exact to the
minute, the day-of-week checks out, and the Sunday-night HBO reading is
the only reading under which 'tonight' makes sense ten weeks before a
concert. This is the index page's purchase-record thesis working as
advertised in miniature: the tweet supplies an order of operations the
ticket row cannot.

## Confidence boundary

The *sequence* (watching → ticket) is a sound inference from the
timestamp. The *causality* ('the concert was bought by somebody already
watching') is the page's gloss, not something the tweet states. Both are
filed here as the page's reading, verified to its evidence rather than
beyond it.
