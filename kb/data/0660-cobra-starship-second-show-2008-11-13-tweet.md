## Why this matters beyond one stub

This is a third independent instance of the same structural gap the index
page already documents: the concert table undercounts attendance relative
to the twitter archive (The Roots 7 Oct, Trans-Siberian Orchestra 15 Nov,
and now Cobra Starship 13 Nov 2008 — all with day-of tweet evidence, all
absent from the table). The generated artist stubs were built from the
table only, so every single-appearance stub from the Orlando era is
suspect in the same direction. The page's 'saw live 1 time' phrasing
should be read as 'the table records 1 appearance', not as an attendance
fact.

## Unresolved

The tweet names no venue or city; the index page does not fix one either.
Whether the show was in Orlando (where he lived) or elsewhere is open.
The co-bill expansion (Hit The Lights, Forever The Sickest Kids) is the
cross-check page's attribution, not the tweet's — plausible from his
abbreviations, but the tweet alone does not name the other acts.
