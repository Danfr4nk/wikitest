The single most useful thing recovered from the legal material, and it is not a
fact about the subject at all.

For three weeks the prior wiki asserted, in the ordinary declarative voice it
used for everything else, that a Combos theft produced a criminal defence, a
diversion programme and a magistrate's hearing. Downstream pages built on it.
Nothing internal caught it — the error was consistent, plausible, and cited
sources that genuinely existed.

Two properties of the correction are worth keeping.

**It was closed by the operator, not by the system.** The revision block says
"per the operator." No amount of internal cross-referencing surfaced it, because
the conflation was upstream of everything that would have checked it: both
matters were real, both were 2015, and the merged account was internally
coherent.

**Nothing was deleted.** "Nothing has been deleted; the conclusions built on the
old framing are corrected in place on the pages that carried them." The wrong
version is still legible, which is why this datum can exist.

This is the concrete case for `testimony`. Had these 497 pages been ingested as
fact, this error would have entered as an event with a lawyer attached to a
snack theft, indistinguishable in shape from the 496 pages that were right.
