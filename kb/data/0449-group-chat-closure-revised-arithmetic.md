## What the check confirms

The page is unusually honest about its own error history: two dated
corrections, one of which (the 2,248 → 4,657 fix) shows the Dec 2025 number
itself was once wrong by half, so the current figure should be read as the
page's best current reading, not a settled count. The 52-day arithmetic
checks out, and the 'most durable severance' framing is arithmetically
consistent with the 127/110 exit/re-engagement figures the page gives. The
July 23 recontact endpoint is corroborated independently by dat:0358.

## What it does not confirm

The 127 exits, 110 re-engagements, 4,657 messages, 299 affirmations and 0
severance signals are all pipeline counts from unheld CSVs — the held corpus
cannot re-derive them without the Annie handle mapping, which this ingest did
not establish (and which would surface third-party PII to do). The rape-narrative
quotation is double-mediated (logs → AI-podcast dramatization → page) and the
page itself marks it; treat it as allegation-in-the-record, not evidence. The
23:54 timestamp and the 'Tuquick debunked the broken-finger claim in-thread'
detail are likewise the page's, not re-verified. The attachment-model
readings (no internal off-switch, halt-condition failure) are synthesis and
are filed here only as the page's stated significance, not as findings.
