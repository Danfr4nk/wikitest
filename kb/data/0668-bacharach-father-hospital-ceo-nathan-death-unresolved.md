## Notes

The father-son link (Paul Bacharach = Jacob Bacharach's father) is the
page's attribution, not something the press items state — same surname,
same town, consistent with the page, but the corroboration is of the
role and dates, not the kinship. The Nathan Bacharach discrepancy is
genuinely minor and the page is right that nothing hangs on it; it is
filed as unresolved because the stated sources (an obituary and a
*Pittsburgh Jewish Chronicle* piece) were not produced and could not be
found independently.
