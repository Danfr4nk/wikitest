## Why the page's own labels matter

This is one of the few synthesis pages that marks its speculative layer
explicitly: the haplogroup interpretations are 'may indicate' chains, and
the Neanderthal-behavioral link is listed under Gaps as needing clinical
validation. The node preserves those hedges rather than promoting any
figure to a finding.
