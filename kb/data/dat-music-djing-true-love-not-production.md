## What this corrects

The wiki's music framing (including
[`syn:gripnotic-revival`](../syntheses/gripnotic-revival.md) and
[`ent:gripnotic`](../entities/gripnotic.md)) treats the *producer*
identity as the held self-concept. Dan's own testimony says the held
identity is DJ-first: production is secondary; the decks are the
instrument; beatmatching is the physical skill with video-game-like
muscle memory; Full Sail (Recording Arts) was the studio version of what
he wanted to do live, in real time, as performance.

This does not erase the alias system or the GRIPNOTIC signal — it
re-seats them: the aliases are DJ brands first, producer brands second.
