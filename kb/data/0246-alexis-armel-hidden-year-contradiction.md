## What the check confirms

The contradiction is real and the page handles it honestly. The two
accounts cannot both be the same event without compression somewhere: one
is dated March 2014 with five months of concealment and an intermediate
Leah Tedesco residence; the other is dated roughly four months before the
January 2015 lease with no Tedesco residence. The page's three candidate
readings — same event remembered at different resolutions, two separate
evictions in as many years, compression in one account — exhaust the
options, and its refusal to adjudicate is the correct call: testimony does
not get to overrule testimony without a primary record, and there is none
here. Neither message export holds a row from 2014, and the Gchat archive's
dated samples stop at 2013-05, so no contemporaneous channel can break the
tie from inside this repository.

late-2015 terminal one, plus the disputed late-2014/early-2015 account. The
