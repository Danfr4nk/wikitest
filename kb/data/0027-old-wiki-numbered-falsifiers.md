A synthesis that states in advance what would kill it, in a form somebody else
can check.

The falsifiers are specific rather than gestural — *a documented instance of
initiating care before symptoms*, *a dose changed for a physical reason rather
than a budget*, *a conversation with the prescriber about a body rather than a
pharmacy*, *a monitoring output leading to a repair*. Any one of them is a
single observation, and the page says which claim each would break.

Two refinements beyond the bare practice.

**It scored a falsifier as landing partially and narrowed rather than
retreated.** The 2017 dental episode is described as "what a falsifier looks
like when it lands partially: the rule survives narrowed, and the narrowing is
stated above rather than argued away."

**It recorded a prediction as leaning without resolving it.** The intake ledger
was assessed against prediction 4 and found to be an input-measurement regime
with no treatment arm — suggestive, but not squarely a health kick, so the page
logs the lean and hands the resolution forward.

This system has `alternatives` and `counterexamples` fields and no equivalent
for a standing falsifier. `pat:audit-strong-on-numbers-weak-on-meaning` carries
its falsification test in prose because there is nowhere structured to put it.
Filed as a datum; the gap is real.
