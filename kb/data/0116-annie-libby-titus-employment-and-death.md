## What this establishes

This is the page's single strongest employment correction and it checks out
where checkable. The identification mechanism is worth naming: operator
testimony named her, Annie's own messages used the married name 'Libby
Fagen', and the death date (13 October 2024) matches the public record
exactly — a three-way lock on identity that would be near-impossible to
fabricate by accident. The timeline also coheres: told-of-brain-cancer in
March, dead in October, the wage claim dying with her, and the next
documented income event five months later (unemployment denial letter,
2025-03-31).

thread, which this repository does not hold. Filed at high because the
identity lock is public-record-strong; the employment details themselves
rest on the page's sources plus operator testimony and are filed as
