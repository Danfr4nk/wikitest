Recorded at **moderate** rather than high confidence, and the reason is a
structural feature of the source rather than a doubt about the storyteller.

The prior wiki names its evidence carefully throughout — a blotter here, a dated
message there — and for this claim it names nothing. It also lists the DA's
identity under its own Gaps, which means it knew the claim was thin and said so.

That self-marking is what makes the page trustworthy as testimony and this
particular claim weak as evidence, simultaneously. Both readings are correct and
the schema is what keeps them apart: a page can be honest and still be the only
thing standing behind a claim.

The corroboration that does exist is oblique — a quoted message describing
unusual effort by counsel — which supports *that a bar was overcome* without
establishing *what the bar was*.
