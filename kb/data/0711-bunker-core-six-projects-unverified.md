## Why the negative result is the finding

repos, commits, READMEs, error logs, screenshots. None exist in a corpus
that preserves Dan's chat.db forensics, his Gumroad toolkit's existence,
and his vibe-coded games. The absence is informative: it bounds the
ecosystem to testimony about intent and scope, exactly the bound the page
draws for itself. This node records that the bound held when tested.
