The strongest evidential claim in the prior wiki's legal material, and worth
isolating precisely because of how strong it looks.

It is a **quotation from a primary document** — a printed court blotter, naming
the subject in full, with an age, a town, three specific charges and a named
magistrate. That is a different kind of claim from a recollection, and the page
carrying it treats it as settling the date.

What must not be lost in adopting it: **the blotter is not in hand.** The
recovered export cites `raw/legal/documents/2015-02_fayette-court-blotter-possession-charges.md`,
and that file was not part of the four-part export. So this datum records that
the prior wiki quoted a clipping, at high confidence, and says nothing about
whether the clipping exists or was transcribed correctly.

The gap is narrow and closeable: a Fayette County MDJS docket search, or the
newspaper archive for February 1995–2015 filings, would replace this with a
first-party datum. Until then the chain is *page → clipping → court*, and only
the first link is verified.
