## What is contemporaneous and what is reconstructed

The blotter (Feb 2015) and the bar policy's effect (the need for
\"wheeling and dealing\" in late-2015 messages) are the contemporaneous
ends. The middle — preliminary hearing, arraignment, the nine months
between filing and the first surviving message about the case — is entirely
undocumented. A REVISED [2026-08-03] block on the page records a useful
failure of the wiki's own inference: it had judged the capture's \"early
2015\" dating \"almost certainly too early\" because a nine-month gap to
the December barracks trips seemed implausible, until the clipping showed
a twelve-month arrest-to-ARD interval is ordinary in Pennsylvania. The
capture's dating was accurate to the month; the correction instinct
over-fired.

## Open

Whether the January/February 2016 fine payments belong to this case, to
the Combos matter, or to both running concurrently is not determinable
from the message record. Whether the six-month probation was completed
\"without incident\" (Dan's only account) and whether the expungement was
ever completed are unknown. The DA's name is not in the record.
