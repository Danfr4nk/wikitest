## The date tension, restated

The page's REVISED note (2026-07-19) did the right thing correcting the
encounter date to the operator's Oct 20 and flagging the tension with the
message's 'last night'. But it computed the tension from a mis-timed
message (Oct 22 22:26 → 'last night' = Oct 21, one day off Oct 20). The
held timestamp (Oct 23 02:26 UTC ≈ Oct 22 22:26 EDT) puts 'last night'
at Oct 22 — two days after the operator's date. Possible readings: the
operator's memory is off by two days (cf. the documented date-displacement
pattern), 'last night' is loose phrasing for 'the other night', or the
encounter and the filming were different nights. The page picks the
operator; the corpus can't adjudicate further.

## The missing third message

Two candidate explanations: the held pull is missing one message from
the thread, or the page's export counted a message the pull didn't
capture. Either way the register claim ('affectionate re-contact…
corroborates the prior intimacy') survives on the two confirmed
messages, and the 'unanswered' claim holds (0 sent by Dan).
