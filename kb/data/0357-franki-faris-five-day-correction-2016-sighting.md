## What this is evidence of

The page is a model of the wiki's own correction machinery: it retired
its own title phrase ('a five-day rebound') on fresh evidence, pinned
down which span belonged to whom, and extended the Alexis-reconciliation
timeline from 'within weeks' to October 2013. The held-corpus checks add
weight where the page is strongest (the December 2015 shorthand texts are
byte-verbatim in the archive) and one genuine loose end (the May 2016
sighting) where it is weakest.

## What it is not

The 2016 sighting is not proof of contact with Franki Faris — only that
Dan saw someone named Franki that afternoon. It is recorded because the
page's 'nothing after 2013' claim is absolute and the held record is not.
