## What the check confirms

Every checkable element of this section verifies. The two last-2013 tweets
are nineteen minutes apart and barbed; the two 2014 survivors exist at the
stated dates with the stated text and status IDs; no @alexisarmel tweet row
postdates 2014-06-17 in the corpus's twitter pages. The arithmetic the page
builds on it — 85 rows total, 2 vs 23, 3.1% vs 24.2%, the 57-handle
all-time high, the 149-day silence — is internally coherent and consistent
with the rows.

This is the section the page itself flags as its most load-bearing
evidence: the first contemporaneous, dated, non-testimonial support for the
\"occupancy without activation\" claim — twenty-four months of continuous
cohabitation with a public register at zero, recorded as it happened, in
years when he was naming more people publicly than in any year of his life.
The verification here does not weaken that; the tweet rows are primary and
exact.

## What it does not establish — the page's own stated limits, confirmed

The silence begins 29 November 2013, roughly three months *before* the
March 2014 eviction of the first account and nine-plus before the window of
the second. A public register that had already stopped cannot be read as a
register that stopped *because* she was being concealed, and the page
correctly refuses that reading. Attachment behaviour is mostly private, and
the private channel is what remains unmeasured: the Gchat archive's dated
samples stop at 2013-05 and neither message export holds a row from 2012,
2013 or 2014. The 2014 *\"Keep filming me in my sleep\"* tweet stands as the
only contemporaneous dated evidence in any readable corpus that she was in
the room at all that year.

## One soft edge

The 17 June 2014 tweet is timestamped 2014-06-17 00:07:12 UTC, which is the
evening of **16 June** in New York. The page quotes \"17 June 2014\" under
its stated UTC-dating convention; the local date is a day earlier. Not an
error under the convention, but worth knowing before quoting the date.
