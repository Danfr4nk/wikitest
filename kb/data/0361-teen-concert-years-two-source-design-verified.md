## What this is evidence of

The strongest claim on the page is not any single show but the method:
a contemporaneous self-report and a later researched reconstruction are
kept in tension with verification status per row, and contradictions are
named rather than resolved by picking a winner. The two hardest-to-fake
rows — the in-joke secret show and the comedy-festival date — both verify
against public records, which raises the trust weight of the surrounding
rows without converting them to confirmed.

## What it is not

The un-verified rows remain unverified. The six 'Still open' rows and the
two CONTRADICTION rows are open precisely because the page refuses to
close them — this node endorses that posture rather than claiming them.
