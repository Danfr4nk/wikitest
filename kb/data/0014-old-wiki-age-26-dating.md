A dating method worth recording separately from the date it produced, because
the method is sound in a way the date is not yet.

A printed age plus a known birth date bounds a twelve-month window, and a
February filing inside that window can only be February 2015. The inference
holds. It rests entirely on `dat:0013`, whose clipping is not in hand — so this
is a valid derivation from an unverified premise, which is a different thing
from a verified date and should not be allowed to read as one.
