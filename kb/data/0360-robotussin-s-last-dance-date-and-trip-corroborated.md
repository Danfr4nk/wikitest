## What this is evidence of

The wiki's weakest-source pages get stronger in an interesting way when
external facts land: the date was public all along, and the trip was a
named shared memory in the held archive years before the 2026 capture
added the drug context. What the captures add is precisely the part that
cannot be corroborated — the capsule count and the drive — which is why
the page's single-source framing is correct and should survive the
partial upgrade.

## What it is not

The corroborated trip is not corroboration of the DXM. The December 2018
retellings — by both parties — treat the outing as pure adventure and
omit the substance context entirely, exactly as the page observes.
