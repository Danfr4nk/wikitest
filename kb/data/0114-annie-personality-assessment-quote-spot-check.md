## Why this matters

The assessment page is a 407-line interpretive edifice, and the load-bearing
question for any such page is whether its evidence quotes are real. They
are: six of seven checked quotes exist verbatim in the message record, four
on the exact date claimed. The two misses are both one-day displacements —
the precise signature of the good-faith misremembering the veracity ledger
already documents (stated certainty ≈ 0.25 actual), not of invention. The
\"Wow. I quit\" / \"I literally fuckig quit\" pair landing 9 seconds apart
on Jan 6 is, if anything, stronger evidence than the page's Jan-5 dating
suggests: the two utterances are one breath.

## What stays open

The 2024-10-19 \"THERE IS NO SITUATION.\" The Eli-confrontation messages
live in the unheld 2024 thread; the held corpus can neither confirm the
date nor deny it. The 2026-01-21 variant (\"There is no situation Dan\")
shows the phrasing is authentically hers, which is corroboration of voice
rather than of the dated claim.
