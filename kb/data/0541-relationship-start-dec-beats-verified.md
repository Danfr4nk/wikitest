## What the check confirms

The period page's chronology of the Alexis exit is sound: the Nov 30
drunkenness, the Dec 1 elation, and the Dec 1 move-out all land on the dates
the page gives them, verbatim.

## What needs fixing on the page

The key-events bullet for the breakup should not present \"I'm in love with
Annie Ulmer\" as Dan's own line. It is Annie's, written as a script for Dan
to perform on someone else. The difference matters for the page's
attachment framing — the coaching-register version is arguably *stronger*
evidence of her pull on him than a declaration would be, but it is not what
the page implies it is. This node is intentionally narrow: the period page
is otherwise one of the best-verified in the queue.
