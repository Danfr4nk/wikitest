## Why this node exists separately from dat:0169 and dat:0171

The eviction notice (0169) and the vape summons (0171) are artifacts with
their own provenance; the exclusion letter is a family instrument whose
history is a correction story. The correction is the point: a 2026
recollection said \"well before\" and \"each received a letter\"; 2018
messages said April 3 and one letter. The page chose the messages. That
choice is the wiki's evidence discipline in action, and it is worth a node
because future synthesis about the exclusion has to inherit the corrected
version, not the original.
