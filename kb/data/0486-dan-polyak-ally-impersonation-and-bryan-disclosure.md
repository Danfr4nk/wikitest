## The caution to keep carrying

The @clandan handle is the page's own example of refusing an attractive
identification: same joke-name, right era, well-known-person register in
both tweets, and the page still files it as a lead because nothing links
the handle to the name. Any downstream use of the 2008 tweets as
Polyak-attested must carry that non-identification.
