## What this does to the page

It upgrades one of the page's four canonical Dan's Law instances from
'inferred' to 'confirmed' on the attribution axis. The timestamp was never
in doubt (the page already had the message text); the attribution was the
hedged part, and the hedge came from a deficient export, not a deficient
record. The deeper archive carries what the shallow scan lost.

## What it does not do

The confirmation covers the Lex-collapse instance and the Casey
attribution. It does not corroborate the page's other three instances
(Seth Ledonne email #3, the J6 quote, the housing-application language) —
the J6 and housing cases carry separate findings.
