## Standing in the record

This is the aspirational pole of the DJ identity: not craftsmanship
(make the best track) but selection deployed as an event (have the
record nobody has heard and break it on the room). It predicts the
measured dispersal — 321 artists, none over 2.6% of the crate — because
a selector hunts heat, not names. The prediction's check lives at L3
([`int:music-selector-corroborated`](../interpretations/int-music-selector-corroborated.md)).
