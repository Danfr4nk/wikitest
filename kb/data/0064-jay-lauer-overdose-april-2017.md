## Confidence is split across the claim's parts

**High:** the date. Dan told four people the same day at four levels of
candour, and the page preserves the one discrepancy (news arrived the
evening of the 10th; "today" on the 11th) rather than smoothing it. The
thread's last message (an unanswered \"Yo\" on 2017-02-11) and the death
eight weeks later are both dated on the record.

**Moderate:** the drug. The page titles it "a heroin overdose" and quotes
Dan saying Jay \"sold his prescription for heroin,\" but no coroner's
finding, obituary, or news item has been sought — the page says so in its
own gaps. The heroin specification rests on Dan's account alone.

**Wiki synthesis, not fact:** "the only documented death attributable to
the drug economy Dan was inside." That is a census claim over the whole
corpus and this node does not re-run the census; it is attributed to the
old wiki, not asserted.

## What the day opened

The 19:09 condolence to Ellen Ulmer that evening is the first message of a
thread that was still running in November 2025 — the tie that outlasted the
Annie relationship itself was opened by a friend's overdose. Recorded here
because the cocaine page treats Lauer's tier as the financially fragile end
of the supply chain, and the death is what that fragility looks like when it
breaks.
