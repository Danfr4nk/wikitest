A one-sentence statement of the distinction this architecture encodes as typed
negative data, written by the system it replaced.

`never_observed`, `explicitly_rejected` and `known_not_to_occur` are three
different claims, and the ledger's silence is unambiguously the first. The prior
wiki said so in place, on the page carrying the numbers, rather than in a
methodology note nobody reads.

Worth recording for two reasons beyond the fact.

It is a **direct counterexample to any reading that the prior system was
epistemically loose**. On its most quantitative page it volunteered the strongest
available argument against over-reading its own data. Whatever else the 497 pages
turn out to contain, that disposition was present.

And it establishes the correct treatment of the ledger's date range. Four units
over two days is not a claim about consumption before 2026-08-30. The instrument
started then. Any pattern node later built on this data has to carry that bound,
or it will quietly convert two days of measurement into a history.
