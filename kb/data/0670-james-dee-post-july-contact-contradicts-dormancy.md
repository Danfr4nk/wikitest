## Why this matters

This is the governance rule working as specified: documented records
override generated metaphor. The page's 'dormancy-not-exit' framing —
including its citation as the first case inside the dormancy synthesis —
was built on an export that ended the story in July. The held corpus
shows the tie was reactivated within weeks and ran for five more months,
with Dan *initiating* (the 09-18 dispensary ask, the 12-23 track share).
'Dormant' misdescribes a channel where both parties kept texting. The
basement confrontation the page places 'after the message dump's
2025-07-21 endpoint' now has 147 messages of surrounding context to be
dated against — the confrontation is no longer floating in an undated
gap but sits inside a documented sequence.

## What this does not change

The July 11 rupture sequence itself is unaffected —
[`dat:0669`](0669-james-dee-rupture-sequence-verified-minute.md). The
rupture happened; the aftermath was not silence. The 12-19 flare-up
('annie and i are back together') is also new timeline evidence for the
Annie reconciliation arc, flagged here for whoever owns that page.

## Mechanism note

The page was written against the dox-scan-era export; the held
messages.csv is a later, fuller pull. Any page whose 'last message' or
'silence is coverage' claims were built on the older export needs the
same re-check — this is a class of staleness, not a one-off.
