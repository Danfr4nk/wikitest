## What this establishes

This is the rare case where the wiki's most theoretically loaded claim —
that a completed concession requires an interlocutor who narrows rather
than escalates *and* a claim with no exceptionality-verdict attached — has
its evidentiary instance sitting in the held corpus with every timestamp
and every word intact. The mechanism the page describes (redefinition move
starved of ground by narrowing) is visible in the transcript itself: her
*\"I didn't say malicious\"* removes the premise his redefinition needed,
and the concession lands 46 seconds later. The falsifier the page states —
*\"a completed concession should require, structurally, an interlocutor
who narrows rather than escalates\"* — now has n=1 with primary backing.

## Caveats

The n=1 the page already acknowledges is real: one 88-second exchange
cannot carry a general law. Note also what the corpus shows the page does
not print: nine minutes later (21:18:51 EDT) Dan re-frames the concession
as a joke (*\"you can call me a love bomber all you want\"*), which reads
as re-absorption of the conceded term rather than the concession holding
as a changed belief — the resolution completed, but the vocabulary did not
stick. Filed here as held-record texture the page's theory section does
not incorporate.
