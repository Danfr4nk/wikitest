## What the check confirms

The March 7–8 fall date, the gurney quote, and the Fred Adams rotation are
mutually consistent across the page, the old wiki, and the held corpus —
three independent instruments agreeing. The March 29 eviction notice, served
mid-vigil by Diane herself, is the estate moving on the property before Fran
was gone; it is the concrete instance of the \"functional sentinels do the
work while the institutional actors manage the assets\" pattern the page's
Why-it-matters section names.

## What it does not confirm

The $15/hour six-months figure is Dan's contemporaneous account as quoted
on the page; no payroll record was checked. The nursing home's name and the
exact date of the first spill (~late 2017, inferred) remain open gaps on the
page itself.
