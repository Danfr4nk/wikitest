## The extension

The page's 'Multi-act show' framing undersells what the record shows:
this was one of the largest emo/pop-punk festivals of the decade, and the
bill the log preserves is the Bamboozle's Sunday headliner day. The page
was written from the log alone and never made the festival identification.
