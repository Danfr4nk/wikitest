## What the check confirms

The thread is real, the scale is right (191 vs the page's ~195), and the
eleven-year span checks out at the year level. The page's quotes are the only
source for the content — but the metadata agreement makes wholesale
fabrication unlikely.

## What stays relayed

Everything inside the thread. The Sept 2021 retrospective is the corpus's
richest first-person account of the pre-2020 political identity, and it is
quoted at length — but if the Facebook export's thread bodies are ever
deposited, the quotes (especially the long \"California Uber Alles\"
passage and the \"vishnu cleansing\" line) are the first things to re-verify
verbatim. The page's own Gaps (whether any collab ever happened; band detail
beyond the setlist) are carried forward untouched.
