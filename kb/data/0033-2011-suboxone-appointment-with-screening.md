The third independent bearing on one question, and the first from a channel that
actually covers the period.

## The question it lands on

The prior wiki's `the-configured-body` concluded that "the one standing medical
relationship in this life is a **logistics** relationship" — a prescriber whose
entire recorded role is moving a script between pharmacies, never a dose, a
taper or a review ([`dat:0025`](0025-old-wiki-prescriber-exists-routing-only.md)).
That reading rested on 2019–2025 messages, and three of its four quotes could
not be verified against the authoritative corpus
([`dat:0028`](0028-prescriber-quotes-partly-unverifiable.md)).

This message is from **2011** and describes something different in kind: a
scheduled appointment, at a place, with **a drug test administered there**. Not a
pharmacy handoff. That is monitored treatment.

## What it does and does not establish

**Does:** in 2011 the regimen involved appointments and urine screening — a
substantive clinical relationship, which the "logistics only" characterisation
does not cover.

**Does not:** that this persisted. Fourteen years separate it from the 2025
material, and a treatment relationship that begins with monitoring and decays
into script-moving is an entirely ordinary trajectory. The prior wiki's reading
may be correct *about the period it looked at* and wrong only as a general
claim.

The honest form is temporal: **monitored in 2011, logistics-shaped by 2025, with
the transition undated.** That is a better object than either page had — and it
exists only because a third channel covered a year the other two did not.

## The methodological point

Neither the prior wiki's census nor the authoritative iMessage corpus surfaced
this. The census ran on a superseded dump; the corpus holds almost nothing for
2011. It took a channel that was independent *and* covered the window.

That is the argument for breadth over depth in source acquisition: the marginal
value of a fourth message extract is near zero, and the marginal value of a
first Facebook thread was a fact nothing else could see.
