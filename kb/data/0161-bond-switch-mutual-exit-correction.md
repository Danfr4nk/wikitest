## Why this is the read's biggest correction

The standing wiki claim was one-sided: Dan leaving Alexis for an
unattached Annie. The read shows two people exiting two relationships in
the same week, with Dan's mother supplying the material incentive for his
side of it. That reframes the entire 2015 origin story from seduction to
transaction — and it is dated to the first 72 hours, which is what makes it
load-bearing for everything the wiki later says about maternal leverage
(`estate-money-spine`, `supply-network`).

## What is still open

The correction's core (mutual exit + maternal brokering) is the read
pass's finding from an export not held here. One of its two direct
quotations was re-verified in the held iMessage corpus (the name list);
the cocaine-and-car line was not. The 'turd boy' identification that
underpins the mutual-exit half is separately filed as
[`dat:0163`](0163-turd-boy-emilio-identification-testimony.md) because its
