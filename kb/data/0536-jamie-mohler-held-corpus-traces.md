## What the check confirms

The Ethan Kray alias is real and long-lived: a Twitter handle in 2012, a
persona Mohler "insisted he be called" in 2018, and a subject of 2017
conversation in the FB thread. Three independent sources, three eras. The
2018 "thank god he's normal again" line also corroborates the page's reading
that the alias phase was a distinct, passing episode. And the page's
2025-09-19 "ms. Jamie Mohler" line replicates to the minute.

## What it corrects

- The page's "undocumented anywhere else in the corpus" for Ethan Kray is
  wrong on two counts (the 2012 tweet and the 2018 iMessage thread). The
  alias now has three documented sightings.
- The page's corpus-record table entry "First documented friend-group
  reference post-transition: 2025-09-19" is superseded: the March 16, 2025
  (EDT) references — "did anyone tell you about mohler yet," "now: jamie,"
  "Is he still a chick?," "he made the turn fast and was very committed" —
  are six months earlier and treat the transition as already-established
  news. The transition date itself (~2023, operator-attested) is untouched;
  what moves is the documentary floor, not the event.
- The October 6, 2025 Jason exchange is not in the held corpus and remains
  attributed to the prior wiki's other sources, not verified here.

## Deliberately not adjudicated

Whether "the turn" (Dan's euphemism, March 2025) maps to the ~2023 date or to
a later disclosure is not determinable from these messages — "Is he still a
chick?" reads as confirming an already-known change, consistent with a 2023
event surfacing in conversation in 2025, but the messages do not date the
event itself.
