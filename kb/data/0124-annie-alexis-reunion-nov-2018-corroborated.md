## The timezone finding, stated as a rule

Across this whole ingest batch, every 'date displacement' that resolved
did so via the UTC-vs-local offset (Casey 13:47 EST = 18:47 UTC; Bryan
22:26 EDT = 02:26 UTC; here, the entire Nov 2018 timeline). **Ingest
rule: when the old wiki and the held corpus disagree on a timestamp by
~4–5 hours, convert before calling it an error.** The wiki's dates are
local Eastern; the corpus is UTC. Genuine displacements (the personality
assessment's Dec 2 vs Dec 3, Jan 5 vs Jan 6) are full 24-hour
differences, not 5-hour ones — the two classes are distinguishable.
