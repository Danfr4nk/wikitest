## Why this is low, not moderate

2013 — filed honestly as memory by the page itself, with the explicit
warning that nothing here is contemporaneous. The July 9–31, 2013
self-typology emails and the August 14, 2013 post-mortem already in the
corpus \"corroborate the window, not the day.\" The 15 Holly Lane address,
the hitchhikers, the ropes-course job, and the fireworks geography are
