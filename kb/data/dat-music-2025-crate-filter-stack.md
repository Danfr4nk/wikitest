## The numbers that constrain every taste claim

These are the load-bearing measurements. Any interpretation of Dan's
current taste that contradicts them is wrong; any that ignores them is
unfalsifiable.

**The filter stack** (what gets in): energy floor ~0.70, median 0.836 —
energy is the admission ticket and genre is costume (30+ genre tags ride
the same floor). Dark charge over cheer: valence 0.322. Mid-fame, not
chart-chasing and not obscurity-posturing: popularity 43. Three BPM
rooms — 125–145 (house corridor), 165+ (DnB wing), sub-100 (halftime
basement) — which Dan's own testimony reframes as dosage forms, not
dance floors ([`dat:music-dance-energy-hits-no-dancing`](dat-music-dance-energy-hits-no-dancing.md)).

**The anti-loyalty signature**: 321 credited artists, 162 labels, none
above 2.6%. This is the song-as-unit axiom in numbers
([`dat:music-song-unit-axiom`](dat-music-song-unit-axiom.md)) — the data
cannot see the 300 awful songs, but it sees he never went back for
seconds.

**The pipeline**: 91.3% of crate tracks were liked first (hear → Like →
crate), superseding the v1 estimate of 42.3%. The canonical liked union
is 1,913 URIs assembled from three incomplete exports; the November
2025 export holds 498 tracks absent from both June 2026 exports. Like
velocity: 88 across seven years (2017–2023), then 1,417 in 2025, 628 in
November alone — the re-entry year.

**The purge**: 191 tracks bulk-liked Sep 29, 2025 and 286 on Nov 8,
2025 — canon artists (Elliott Smith, Paramore, All Time Low, Lana Del
Rey, Bright Eyes) — then unliked. Interpreted as the old-identity
metadata self being re-auditioned song by song and mostly deleted; the
interpretation is medium-high confidence, not testimony.

**The crate as ammunition, not canon**: 60% 2025 releases, built in
seven weeks, zero overlap with the 5,321-track history. Four waves
(Oct 31: 170; Nov 21: +45; Dec 9: +16; Dec 22: +1); the late 64 went
housier; one version swap (HOOLIGANS Ghastly-feature replacing solo),
one outright cut (ELECTRIC LOVE). Top Songs 2025 overlaps only 45/100 —
DJ-functional selection, not play ranking.

**The gap**: monthly playlists 2011–2020 (HOLIDAY 2020 = 46 tracks),
then no 2021–2024 titled entries — the 41-month Au Za'atar block. The
supported reading is diminished curatorial habit, not stopped listening;
causation is unproven.
