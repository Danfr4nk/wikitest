## Why the sourcing matters

The page is unusually careful about this — it names the figure as a
self-estimate and contrasts it with the scored instrument on the other
side of its CONTRADICTION block (Impulsiveness at the 96th percentile, a
real measurement). The finding to preserve is the asymmetry: one side of
the page's central contradiction is a scored instrument, the other is a
man describing his own engine in August 2026. The page holds both; so does
this node. Neither is discarded, and neither is upgraded.

## What would move it

A persistence or conscientiousness-facet score from a standardized
instrument, or a dated behavioral rate (completions per pursuit) derived
from the record, would turn the estimate into a measurement. Neither is in
this repository.
