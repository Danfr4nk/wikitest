## Why this is load-bearing

This is a present-tense first-person report of his own perception — the
highest provenance available for an interior state. It is the mandatory
first frame for all playlist/music analysis: vocals are
cadence/phonetics/tone/melody/processing/rhythm/texture/energy, never
proposition. Spotify `speechiness` therefore reads as
voice-as-instrument density, not wordiness.

It also answers the open question in
[`syn:gripnotic-revival`](../syntheses/gripnotic-revival.md) about the
thirteen-year "never sung original lyrics" pattern: sung originals were
never in the possibility space, because lyrics do not resolve into
meaning for him at all.

The ~3 exceptions remain unnamed (asked, unanswered 2026-09-11).
