Two claims share one sentence on the source page and they are not the same kind
of claim.

**That a refusal occurred** is reported observation. The exchange is in a chat
export; someone said "no comment."

**That the refusal means the supply is unmanaged** is inference from silence,
and the prior wiki did not merely relay it — it certified it, with the word
*correctly*.

The distinction matters downstream because the second claim does not stay put.
The same sentence continues into consequence: the unmanaged supply becomes "a
live, tracked threat feeding a second, parallel layer of the same vigilance
loop." So a refusal to answer a question has, inside one sentence, become a fact
about a supply arrangement and then a component of a psychological mechanism.

Filed as a datum because the assertion is real and checkable — the page says
this. Whether the inference holds is a separate question, taken up at L3 in
[`int:inference-from-refusal-is-unsound`](../interpretations/inference-from-refusal-is-unsound.md).
