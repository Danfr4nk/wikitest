## What the check confirms

The claim chain is intact: concert-log entry, performer page and master event
list all agree on date, venue and \"Confirmed.\" status, and the attendance
side is independently dated by the 22:54 Vine post (see
[`dat:0152`](0152-chappelle-oddball-2013-08-30-appearance.md)). The tour-level
public record confirms Hannibal Buress was on the 2013 Oddball bill and that
the Pittsburgh stop happened on this date at the named venue — so the
concert log's Pittsburgh-specific claim is consistent with, not contradicted
by, the public record.

## What it does not confirm

Three things were not directly read here: the Vine post and tweet (twitter
archive on Drive, not pulled), the ticket/concert log itself (table.csv not
held), and the 2026 retelling (thirteen years post-hoc). The iMessage corpus
says nothing either way. And the \"saw perform live\" phrasing assumes he
watched Buress's set: Oddball ran two stages, so attendance at the festival
is firmer than attendance at any one set.

## Naming note

The wiki leads with \"Post-Gazette Pavilion (First Niagara Pavilion)\";
the contemporaneous June–August 2013 tour press uniformly names the venue
**First Niagara Pavilion**, matching the page's parenthetical rather than
its lead. Filed as observed, not as a correction — the parenthetical
already carries the right name.
