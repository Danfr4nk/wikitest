## Why the title matters to the chain

If the Virginia Avenue passage is in *The Bend of the World* (2014),
then Dan moved into the book's setting (January 2015) after the book
existed — the sequence the page currently carries. If it is in
*Doorposts* (2017), the same 'moved in after publication' sequence still
holds (tenancy began January 2015, book published 2017), so the timeline
of the coincidence survives either way. What changes is only which shelf
the evidence sits on — and five years of the wiki calling it by the wrong
title. The open item is small and precisely scoped: check pages 227–228
of a physical *Bend of the World* against the Virginia Avenue passage.
