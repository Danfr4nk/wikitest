## What the check confirms

The page did not misdescribe Aug 26. The collapse to 0–1 messages/day on
Aug 23–26, after five days of triple-digit rupture traffic, is the shape a
real block (or a real cold stop) makes in the record, and the page's
characterization — \"the block is not currently being used as a dramatic
event\" — matches the traffic shape better than a theatrical cutoff would.

## What the check resolves

The page asked to be tested over time, and the test ran: by Aug 29 the
thread is back at 108 messages/day, two-sided, in the crisis register.
This is the page's falsifier #1 firing cleanly, four days after the
observation. It does not retroactively falsify the Aug 26 report — the page
was careful to date itself — but it closes the page's open question in the
negative: the August rupture did not, in the event, produce \"a stable
behavioral boundary that does not require emotional warfare to maintain.\"
The very next week required exactly that.
