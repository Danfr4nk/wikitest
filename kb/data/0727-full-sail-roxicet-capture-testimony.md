## Why low confidence is not a dismissal

The account's specificity (thirty count, daily route, exact boundary
dates) is the kind of detail that usually comes from a real memory rather
than a constructed one — but specificity is not corroboration, and the
page itself refuses to upgrade it. This node follows the page's lead: the
claim is preserved in full, labeled testimony, and left for any future
primary source (dental records, Spetch's own account) to move. The
Suboxone start date is load-bearing for the health record and is kept
visible as the operator's consistent long-term account, not as a verified
medical fact.
