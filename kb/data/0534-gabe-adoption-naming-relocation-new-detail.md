## The MAX_PRIME contradiction

The 2026-era bootloader referring to a cat dead since October 2023 in the
present tense is either stale text or evidence the date is wrong. dat:0292's
corpus record makes the second horn much weaker — the contemporaneous
"I had to put gabe down today" is a first-person timestamped record, which
outranks a bootloader's tense. The likelier reading is the page's first: the
bootloader text is older than its last-edited date. Either way the date side of
the contradiction is closed.
