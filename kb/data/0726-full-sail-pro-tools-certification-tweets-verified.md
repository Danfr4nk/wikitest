## What the sequence proves and what it doesn't

It proves the certification happened January 26, 2010, after a
near-miss the day before, and that Dan announced the Brooklyn move ten
and a half minutes after announcing the pass. It does not prove the
certification *caused* the move — the page's 'so the move was already in
motion; the certification was the green light' reading is plausible
sequencing, stated as the page's inference. The inference is reasonable;
it is still an inference, and this node keeps the two tiers apart.
