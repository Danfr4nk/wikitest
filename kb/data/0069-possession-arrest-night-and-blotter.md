## Why this is the load-bearing legal node

Three properties make it the corpus's strongest legal datum: (1) the
charges are confirmed by a contemporaneous third-party document, not by
testimony; (2) the date is fixed by arithmetic (age 26 on the blotter)
rather than by recollection; (3) the account of the night comes from a
2026 operator capture told with no access to the clipping, and the charge
list still matches exactly.

## What it separates this arrest from

Until 2026-08-03 the wiki filed this event's lawyer, ARD and hearing under
the Combos retail theft (see
[`dat:0072`](0072-retail-theft-arrest-split-from-possession.md)). Both are
real; only this one was an arrest. The split is the reason this page
exists.

## Open

The exact arrest date within the days before February 17, 2015, the
