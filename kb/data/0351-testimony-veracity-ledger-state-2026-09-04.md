## What this ledger is, and what it is not

It is the wiki's standing scorecard on its subject's testimony: ten
settled claims, each with the evidence that settled it and the arithmetic
over them. The page's own framing is precise about its limits — n=10, a
filtered (non-random) sample, one living person excluded by a standing
directive (which it discloses as a bias rather than hiding), and 'Nothing
here measures honesty.'

## Why t013's staleness matters

The ledger is the instrument the ingest governance rules lean on ('stated
certainty ≈ 0.25 actual'). Its numbers are unchanged by the operator's
correction — a correction that *confirms* the ledger's verdict on t013
while *overturning its stated reason*. That is exactly the
[`pat:reasoning-sound-provenance-unreliable`](../patterns/reasoning-sound-provenance-unreliable.md)
shape dat:0058 names. The ledger cannot be hand-edited ('regenerated by
`bin/wiki-testimony page` and overwritten on every write'), so the fix is
a regeneration that re-adjudicates t013 with the audit/labs/certification
account in the 'what settled it' column.
