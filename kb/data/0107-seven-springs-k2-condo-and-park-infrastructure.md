## What the check confirms

The **unit K2** detail is the strongest claim on the page: two independently
written 2026 captures (the 08-01 bald-eagle-cummings capture behind dat:0209
and the 08-09 extreme-sports capture) converge on the family holding unit K2
in the Villages at Seven Springs, ski-in-ski-out, with dat:0209 adding the
prior-rental history. The **Wallisch thread** is the next strongest: the
public record independently confirms every load-bearing element of the
capture's framing — Pittsburgh-born, Seven Springs as the home hill, 4bi9
Media as the crew that produced his 2007 breakout video.

## What it leaves open

The park-inventory numbers (12-foot pipe, 3–4 features, several dozen by
2006) and the Nuttings-takeover causal claim are single-source and
unconfirmed. They are precise enough to be checkable against Seven Springs'
own resort-development history, which nobody here has done. Filed as
unverified with the reason stated — the numbers are the operator's recall,
not resort records.
