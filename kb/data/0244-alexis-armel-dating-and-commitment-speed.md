## What the check confirms

The operator's 2026 account of the meeting survives contact with the
contemporaneous record intact: in PA for Thanksgiving (the 2009-11-25
*\"pennsylvania, what's a stoner to do?!\"* and the unnamed \"her\" of
29–30 November), named publicly for the first time on 14 December from
Orlando, \"girlfriend\" two days later. The page's own summary of the
corroboration is exact, and the testimony-ledger entry (t012) independently
scores the same capture 'confirmed' against the same tweet sequence.

The tattoo dating is the hardest in the corpus for this relationship: three
tweets, sixteen minutes, a Saturday night in January, eight weeks after they
met. The page corrected its own \"four months\" to eight weeks on 2026-09-02;
the corrected figure is what the archive carries.

## What it leaves open

The capture also supplies the causal story the corpus had never had — the
Thanksgiving relapse (thirty Roxicet pills, \"the opiate addiction that Dan
had truly kicked while in college is back in force\"), the Orlando supply
line via Spetch, escalation over Christmas, Suboxone stabilization seven
weeks later on February 17, 2010. None of that is in the tweets, and no
other source in this repository holds it. It is single-source 2026
testimony about 2009, corroborated on the relationship's shape and pace but
not on the drug chain. Filed as attributed testimony, not established fact.

## A note on the \"August 2009\" graduation inside the capture

The 2026-08-27 capture dates the meeting to \"a few months after the August
2009 Full Sail graduation.\" The operator corrected the graduation month to
**September 2009** on 2026-09-09 ([`dat:0058`](0058-graduation-september-2009-then-audit-and-certification.md)).
The Thanksgiving-2009 meeting date is unaffected — the capture's sequencing
(Thanksgiving trip, a few months post-graduation) holds under either month —
but the capture's stated month is now stale and should not be quoted as the
graduation date.
