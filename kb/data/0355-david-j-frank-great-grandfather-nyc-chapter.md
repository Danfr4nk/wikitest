## What this is evidence of

A clean example of the wiki's own data hygiene: the page both uses the
GEDCOM extraction and warns against trusting it, naming the specific
bad row (1993–1997 Uniontown) so it cannot be cited downstream. The
NYC-years correction is the substantive move — it changes the
fayette-return synthesis from 'an immigrant arriving is not a person
returning' to 'the founder chose Fayette over New York at the first
opportunity', which makes Dan's own NYC→Fayette moves a repetition
rather than a retreat.

## What it is not

The census rows themselves are not verified here. US census records are
public and a future pass could check David J. Frank (b. 12 Aug 1892,
Russia) in the 1900/1910 Manhattan and 1915 Bronx schedules directly —
that would promote this from derived-from-unheld to independently
corroborated.
