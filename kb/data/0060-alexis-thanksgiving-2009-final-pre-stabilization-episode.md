## Split verdict: the relationship is corroborated, the drug chain is not

The corroboration table on the Alexis page (integration dated 2026-09-02,
after the tweet archive became available) confirms every load-bearing
*relationship* element of the testimony — the unnamed "her" of 29–30 November
preceding the first named tweet by a fortnight matches the "week or two after
that he buys her a one-way ticket" account, and the Christmas trip details
align. That is testimony surviving contact with an independent artefact.

What does not survive it: the Roxicet pills, Spetch, the Winter-Park-to-Ocala
supply run, and the one-way ticket purchase. Those appear nowhere in the
tweets. They are a 2026 recollection of 2009, seventeen years after the fact,
from a single source. The drug chain is therefore **unverified** — plausible,
uncorroborated, and recorded here as testimony rather than fact.

## What this changes on the chemical-architecture page

Nothing about the headline figure moves: the sixteen unbroken years are
measured *from* February 17, 2010, and the episode is supplied as the named
cause of the previously undated, causeless "stabilization at Full Sail's
end." The page is careful about this and this node preserves the care: the
episode *precedes* the measured window, it does not alter it.
