documents — this repository cannot re-derive the 299, the 187-of-191, the
24 alibis or the 44 caretaking-crisis answers. What the page *does* give
this repository is its own audit trail: a dated correction that names the
exact failure mode (citing the pre-audit figure from the document that

because the omission was *\"directly relevant to this model's central
claim.\"* That is the correction machinery working on its own output, and
it is checkable at the level of text-fidelity even though the underlying
counts are not. Cross-reference [`dat:0118`](0118-annie-page-number-table-self-corrections.md),
which covers the Annie page's own (2026-08-20) corrections and the
