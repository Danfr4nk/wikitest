## Why this is filed despite the journey adding no fact

The journey's thesis is itself the finding: *each instrument is a
correction or a generalization of the one before it, and every one
outgrew its target.* That is a second-order claim about the corpus's
method, stated once, on this page, and it is the page's own contribution
rather than any stop's. The numbers are carried for reference so a future
session does not re-extract them from the wrong page.

## The load-bearing example

The block-unblock-loop's failed prediction is the corpus's worked example
of its own rule — a falsified conclusion kept visible rather than deleted
(per STYLE_GUIDE). The journey ends there deliberately: it is the
instrument layer's three rules ([`dat:0076`](0076-instrument-layer-three-rules.md))
demonstrated on the corpus's most-watched prediction.
