## What the reversal would change if it holds

The supply-network page's single-point-of-failure reading of May 2026 —
Tom no-shows, withdrawal follows — becomes a two-line architecture: the
prescription is the default, the dealer network is the recovery path, and
May 2026 is the failover failing with the primary already down. That
matches the page's prediction 2 ("the Suboxone supply will fail again
through geography, not through Tom"). It is a materially different
architecture and the page is right to flag it as a contradiction rather
than absorb it.

## What keeps it from being a finding yet

The quoted June 2025 messages are not in the authoritative corpus
(dat:0028), and the corpus has documented coverage holes. The census that
surfaced them ran on a superseded dump (dat:0025). Until the 2025-06-12/13
sequence is located in a verifiable export — the facebook archive ends
2022-09 and does not cover it — the reversal is an open adjudication, not
a verdict. The page itself records it as a flagged contradiction, and this
node preserves that status rather than promoting it.
