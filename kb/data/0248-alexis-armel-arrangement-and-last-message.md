## What the check confirms

The page's own provenance handling here is careful and is preserved: the
$3,000 figure and the \"plaything for Annie\" phrasing are Dan's words
about his own arrangement, told to Emaly while he was pursuing her — the
page names the motive context rather than laundering it. The WEXUS

message CSV. The terminal date (2025-02-19) is the page's assertion about
