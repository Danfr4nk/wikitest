## What the check confirms

Three independent confirmations of the attendance claim: the artist page itself,
the master event list row 26, and concertarchives.org all put the same six-band
lineup (Panic! At the Disco, Coheed and Cambria, Paramore, Gym Class Heroes,
Cobra Starship, The Academy Is...) at Meadowlands Sports Complex, East
Rutherford NJ on May 4, 2008. The old wiki's researched timeline independently
calls it The Bamboozle 2008. The date falls on the festival's second day
(concertarchives: May 3–4, 2008). **The attendance is high-confidence.**

## What it corrects

The artist page's framing — *"Panic! at the Disco & Coheed and Cambria
co-headlined"* — is misleading. This was not a two-band bill; it was the
second day of a ~140-band festival. The operator's log recorded the two bands
he cared about and the rest of the bill as "also", and the prior wiki passed the
framing through uncorrected. The "Panic! & Coheed headline" label in the wiki's
own researched timeline is a shorthand, not a billing claim — Concert Archives
shows both bands listed among dozens.

Note on scope: [`dat:0240`](0240-bamboozle-2011-edition.md) covers The Bamboozle
*2011*; there was no prior node for the 2008 edition. This row constitutes the
repository's first evidence of 2008 attendance.
