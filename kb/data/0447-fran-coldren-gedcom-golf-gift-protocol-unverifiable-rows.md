## What the check confirms

Only one thing in this group could be checked, and it held: the
'gram did aaaaaanything' summary line is real, dated exactly two days after
her death, matching the page's framing. The age-97 resolution is
arithmetically consistent with the birth and death dates the page gives, even
though the single message the page rests it on is not in the held corpus.

## What it does not confirm

The genealogy is the weakest link on this page by source type: a GEDCOM from
an unheld zip, read by the page's author (an AI session per the changelog's
'GEDCOM pass'), with operator captures filling the narrative around it. The
three-marriage table, the 1940-census Smithfield placement, the 57-year
Belmont tenure, and the next-door adjacency of Annie's grandparents are all
filed here as the page's claims, not established facts. The golf figures and
the gift protocol are similarly single-sourced to captures. The 'Fred Adams
authorised the pay' claim and the 'keep me at home' directive are negative
findings — absent from the held corpus, present on the page from an unheld
dump — and should be cited with that split explicit. Anyone acting on the
Fran genealogy (estate questions, the 57-year tenure, the Whyel money trail)
needs the GEDCOM itself, which is not in this repository.
