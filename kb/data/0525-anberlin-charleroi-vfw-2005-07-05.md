## What the check confirms

The July 5, 2005 Charleroi VFW row is internally consistent across every old-wiki
location that carries it: date, venue, city, lineup (Saosin, Anberlin, Acceptance,
Terminal, Codeseven), tour name ("All Dudes All the Time Tour") and the
"Resolved." qualifier all agree. The Anberlin page's second appearance (Jul 29,
2008 Warped Tour) matches [`dat:0241`](0241-vans-warped-tour-four-editions.md)
exactly and is not re-filed here.

## What it does not confirm

All four internal witnesses are the same instrument — the unattested concert
log, relayed through the prior wiki. "Resolved." is the log's own verdict with
no surviving evidence of what was resolved (the show was presumably a club date
in a small PA town — the kind of thing that would leave little public record).
A web search found no independent listing of the Charleroi VFW stop; the tour
itself is real (concertarchives.org carries same-lineup 2005 dates), but that
corroborates the tour, not this stop.
