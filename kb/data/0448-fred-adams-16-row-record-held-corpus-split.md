## What the check confirms

The page's core methodological claim — that sixteen dated contemporaneous
messages name Adams — is half-verifiable in the held corpus and the verified
half is exactly quoted. The 2020-08-06 'talked to jasón adams' row is the most
important of the verified five: it is the page's evidence that Jason, not
Fred, handled the inheritance signing, and it closes the gap dat:0291 left
open. The absent rows fall in export windows the held corpus demonstrably
lacks (the Suz thread has no 2016-06 or 2020-05 rows at all), so this is a
corpus-window limitation, not a page fabrication — the old-wiki-export of
2026-09-04 preserves them.

## What it leaves open

The death report itself (Suz, 2020-05-24) remains unverified in the held
corpus, as dat:0291 found; the death window 18–21 May 2020 rests on the
page's reading. The Rick 2018-01-06 category message, the 13 March 2018
no-going-home decision, and the Coldren Adams → Adams & Adams evolution are
likewise old-wiki-export-only. The estate docket is still the missing
document: it would date Adams's death, name the August 21 signing judge, and
settle whether the distribution's timing had anything to do with the valve
losing its centre. Extends (not duplicates) dat:0291 on the 2020-08-06 row.
