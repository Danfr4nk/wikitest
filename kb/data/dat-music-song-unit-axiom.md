## Standing

Stated as an axiom, not a preference — the same register as his
load-bearing psychology axioms. Each track is evaluated in isolation;
artist, era, genre, and narrative context are contamination, not
information. This is the philosophical license for the selector
archetype: if songs are severed from metadata, a DJ can play anything
that clears the filter, with no cool-points accounting.

It converges with his broader cognitive architecture (explicit,
atomized, compartmentalized judgments over blended impressions) — a
convergence noted at L3, not asserted here.
