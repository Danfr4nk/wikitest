## Why this is a correction, not a corroboration

(Plutarch, Goldsworthy Roman) to immediate present\" — dates the page now
says are real but are \"two closed sets, not a tour of the library.\" The
author head corroborates [`dat:0479`](0479-bob-woodward-five-books-no-five-star.md):
Woodward 5 and Wolff 5 appear on both pages — but it is the same single
source cited twice, not an independent measurement. Goldsworthy 4 and Karl 3
are first recorded on this page (their own author pages are outside this
worker's list).
