## What the arc is and is not

The arc is the single best-documented case in the corpus of
money-as-the-independent-variable in substance consumption, but its
documentation is one retrospective reconstruction, not a ledger. Every
figure above this node comes from recollection: the wiki is explicit
that on the night of 2026-08-30 the ledger recorded the first quantity
"written down as it happened rather than reconstructed afterwards."

That one measured night (0.75 g over 9h 31m — see
[`dat:0063`](0063-intake-ledger-first-measured-night.md)) lands inside
the stated 0.5–1 g/day post-2020 band, so the first measurement does
not contradict the recollection. That is worth something, and it is the
whole of it.

## What would firm it up

[`dat:0024`](0024-old-wiki-happiness-counter-measure.md) already puts an
outcome measure beside the peak window (0.87/0.34/0.41 happiness claims
per 1,000 messages in 2018–2020 vs 7.86 in late 2015). What remains
missing per the page itself: dollar figures for the 3.5–7g/day peak, and
any rate at all for the post-2020 band — one unit cannot say how many
nights like it there are in a week.
