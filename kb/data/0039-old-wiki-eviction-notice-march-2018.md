## The prior wiki's own reasoning, which is careful

It had recorded the tenancy as running to February 2019 and the notice's outcome
as undocumented. A court summons then placed Dan's address of record at a
different house seven days after the notice — and the page did **not** conclude
he had moved.

Its stated reason is worth preserving verbatim in substance: *an address on
court paper is an address **of record**, which can lag or lead a place of
sleep.* It notes a third house in use across the same spring, and settles on the
narrow claim that **the tenancy's paper life ended in March 2018 while its lived
life may not have.**

That is the correct shape. A document establishes what a document says.
