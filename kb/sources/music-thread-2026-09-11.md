## What it supplies (verbatim, abridged)

1. **Lyric qualifier:** "I do not hear the lyrics of songs and even if I
   have a lyric sheet to know the words interpreting them is harder yet.
   I hear the lyric as timbre. There are literally like 3 songs whose
   lyrics I actually have parsed as a conceptual message."
2. **DJ as true love:** "DJing is my true love not production. DJing is
   like the physical manifestation of my internal music experience with a
   video game like muscle memory / rhythmic beat match physical element.
   It was like what I went to college for but done in real-time and as a
   performance."
3. **Selector archetype:** "the dj'ing I wanted to do was all about
   being the source of absolute smash fire tracks that no one knows yet.
   Diplo, DJ shadow, etc."
4. **Dance-music function:** "The dance music i like, because I haven't
   and would never dance ever omg, is literal energy hits through
   auditory stimuli."
5. **Song-as-unit axiom:** "music is songs. A good song is good,
   regardless of who made it. Lots of people make 1 great song and 300
   awful ones. Death of the artist. Compartmentalize. A song is the unit
   and is disconnected from the metadata."
6. **Timeline:** trap era 2013–14 (the SLOPPP era); 2025 re-entry into
   the exclusively-dance-music world.
7. **Annie overlap (his framing):** the Annie decade (Nov 2015–Aug 2026)
   ≈ the fallow decade for dance-world participation; the 2025 re-entry
   lands on the 2025 collapse (Feb–Apr 2025: NYC exit, Annie's move to
   her parents).

Item 7 is explicitly his framing, not a measured causal claim. Items 1–5
are present-tense reports of his own phenomenology and philosophy — the
highest-provenance material available for interior states.
