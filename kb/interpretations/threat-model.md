## The thesis

His stated threat model — **"competence correctly deployed, outcome still
catastrophic"** — is the load-bearing sentence of his cognitive
self-portrait. The corpus's profile reads him as Ti-dominant forensic
analyst (reality as a high-fidelity system; "find where it breaks"),
Ne pattern-hunting, Si archival recall, Fe-inferior — and names the
failure mode as the **diagnosis-to-behavior gap**: never ignorance, always
the distance between seeing the mechanism and acting on it.

The interpretation: the threat model does double duty. Descriptively, it
names a real pattern — he does see mechanisms clearly and does still lose.
But functionally, it is **identity equipment**: it converts every
catastrophe into evidence of the model's truth rather than evidence
against his competence. "I saw it coming" is preserved no matter what
happens. The model is unfalsifiable by design — a good outcome proves
competence, a bad outcome proves the threat model — and unfalsifiable
equipment should be handled as equipment, not as theory.

## The evidence for the descriptive reading

- **The BFS incident shift is the confirming instance.** On the shift he
  ran a correct expected-value calculation on the ~$35 Suboxone stake
  ("positive EV given how high the utility of a strip was in his state"),
  independently identified the gas-lady fraud vector (prepay drive-off via
  no-dispense void), and diagnosed the drawer procedure as the exploit
  ("last documented count = the person on the hook"). Competence,
  correctly deployed, in real time, under withdrawal. He still lost the
  job over a disputed $50 ([`dat:0496`](../data/0496-bfs-foods-job-and-drawer-dispute.md),
  [`evt:2026-05-bfs-firing`](../events/2026-05-bfs-firing.md)).
- **The estate spine correction is the model working on itself.** The
  2026-08-18 direction reversal — capital ran Dan-to-Suz, the $750/week
  figure retracted — is the forensic instrument catching its own error
  and publishing the correction with dated blocks
  ([`dat:1293`](../data/1293-estate-spine-direction-reversal-dan-to-suz.md)).
  Diagnosis applied to his own record, behavior changed (the page now
  reads the other way).
- **The audit's boundary is the model's blind spot, named.** The deviance
  audit is "an instrument reasoning about its own commissioner"
  (dat:0891) — the one place the forensic stance looks at the forensics.
  The threat model has no such move built in: there is no recorded
  instance of "competence incorrectly deployed" in his telling.

## The evidence for the equipment reading

- **The failure mode is always the gap, never the diagnosis.** Across the
  corpus, when things go wrong, the story is that he saw it and couldn't
  act (BFS: saw the supply failure coming, ran out of options), never that
  he misread the situation. A threat model that never assigns error to
  perception is protecting the perceiver.
- **The model absorbs its own counterevidence.** The BFS sequence
  correction killed the retaliation theory — a case where his initial
  *diagnosis* (protected refusal → retaliation) was wrong, and the
  instrument caught it. That is a misdiagnosis on the record, and the
  threat model as stated has no sentence for it; the correction was filed
  as a triumph of the instrument rather than a failure of the diagnosis.
- **The type attribution is self-sourced.** The Ti-dominant reading rests
  substantially on his own self-typing (2024-11-04 "INTP 5w6sx RLOEI" in
  the held corpus; the categorical Ti-dominance re-derivation at
  dat:0890 ran 13/0 on a held slice — real, but on his own text). A threat
  model derived from a self-assigned type is a story the type tells about
  itself, and types flatter.
