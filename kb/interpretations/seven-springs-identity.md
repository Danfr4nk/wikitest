## The thesis

Seven Springs ski resort (~25 miles from Uniontown) is the corpus's last
clean pre-rupture identity site: every-weekend adolescent social world,
stronger than his high-school friendships, family condo unit K2 in the
Villages at Sunridge, ski-in-ski-out, trampoline training as off-season
prep, the resort's 2006 terrain-park build-out happening inside his exact
window of living it ([`dat:0107`](../data/0107-seven-springs-k2-condo-and-park-infrastructure.md)).
The K2 detail is the hardest claim on the page — two independently written
2026 captures converge on it — and the Tom Wallisch thread (Pittsburgh-born,
Seven Springs as home hill, 4Bi9 Media's 2007 breakout) checks against the
public record.

The same site, in the same capture, holds the complication: per his own
2026-08-09 retrospective, the ski crew was his first drug-exposure cohort
and his first cocaine use is dated to **2004** — a full year before the
November 2005 hinge (father's rehab disclosure, parents' divorce, the
corpus's stated life-rupture;
[`evt:2005-11-parental-rupture`](../events/2005-11-parental-rupture.md)).
The clean story the rest of the corpus tells — the divorce caused it —
breaks on the identity site's own evidence: **the place that made him is
also the place that exposed him.** The hinge narrative survives as the
meaning he assigned the rupture; it does not survive as the causal story of
first use.

## The evidence for

- **K2 is doubly attested.** Two separate 2026 captures (the 08-01
  bald-eagle-cummings capture behind dat:0209 and the 08-09 extreme-sports
  capture) agree on unit K2, Villages at Seven Springs, ski-in-ski-out,
  with the earlier adding prior-rental history (dat:0107). This is the
  strongest factual anchor in the Seven Springs material.
- **The 2004 date is his, stated twice.** The 2004/ski-crew dating is the
  page's headline claim from the dictated 2026-08-09 capture (dat:1393).
  The later self-audit adds the instrumental framing — first cocaine use as
  a weight-control tool tied to adolescent bulimia — as much as social,
  which cuts against the pure social-contagion reading and complicates the
  hinge story from a second direction.
- **The hinge is meaning, not mechanism.** November 2005 remains his
  stated life-hinge event. The interpretation doesn't dispute that; it
  disputes that first use follows from it. A rupture can be the central
  event of a life without being the first cause of its habits.

## The evidence against

- **The page carries its own internal tension.** Its connection block lists
  both 2004 and age 17–18 (2005–2006) framings for first cocaine use
  (dat:1393). The 2004 headline is not the page's only dating — the
  tension this node is built on is acknowledged inside the source.
- **Everything is 2026 retrospective.** The 2004 date, the crew-as-cohort
  claim, the instrumental framing, the K2 condo, the park build-out — all
  from captures dictated 20+ years later, carried as testimony
  (dat:1393, dat:0107). His documented failure mode is date displacement,
  not dishonesty — 2004 could easily be 2005, and a one-year slip moves
  first use to the hinge's doorstep.
- **The held corpus is silent on all of it.** No primary row anchors the
  Seven Springs years, the crew, or the first use. The interpretation is
  built on testimony about testimony.

## What the ingest changed

The ingest filed the tension honestly rather than resolving it: dat:1393
carries the 2004/ski-crew headline *and* the page's internal 2004-vs-17–18
contradiction; dat:0107 separates the doubly-attested K2 from the
single-source park numbers and the Nuttings causal claim. The honest
statement is the stratified one: condo K2 is well-supported, first-use
dating is contested inside its own source, and the hinge-vs-exposure
tension is real on the record as it stands.

## Open questions

- How long did the family keep the condo; did skiing continue past
  adolescence? (Gaps per dat:1393.)
- Named individuals from the crew — the cohort is described, never
  populated.
- Whether the bulimia/instrumental framing (self-audit) predates or
  postdates the 2004 social dating in his own telling — the two framings
  may belong to different self-audits.
