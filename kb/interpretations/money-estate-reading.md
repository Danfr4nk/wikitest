## The thesis

Fran Coldren's estate paid Dan six advances totalling $42,500 between
October 2019 and June 2020, and a final share of $144,069.31 gross
($134,069.31 after the June advance), available 2020-09-01
([`evt:2026-estate-advances`](../events/2026-estate-advances.md),
[`dat:0051`](../data/0051-estate-advances-both-pages-were-right.md)). The
prior wiki's corpus-level reading of what that money *did* is the
finite-windfall arc: the cocaine dosage curve tracks the inheritance —
~1g/day baseline to 3.5–7g/day while the capital constraint was off
(2017–2020), back to ~0.5–1g/day as it was exhausted
([`dat:0062`](../data/0062-cocaine-dosage-arc-finance-driven.md)) — and the
retrospective's own conclusion is that the "desired state" is maximum
intensity limited only by supply and money, not by any internal ceiling.

The interpretation: **the money priced intensity, not rescue.** A windfall
large enough to remove the capital constraint on consumption did not fund
moderation, investment, or exit; it funded the unconstrained rate, and the
contraction afterward was a resource adjustment, not a step toward recovery.
That is the corpus's clearest case of money-as-independent-variable in
substance consumption — and the old wiki stated it that way itself.

## The evidence for

- **The arc's shape.** 1g → 3.5–7g → 0.5–1g, with the peak window exactly
  the inheritance window. The one measured night (2026-08-30: 0.75g over
  9h31m) lands inside the post-2020 band — the first primary measurement
  does not contradict the retrospective (dat:0062).
- **The happiness counter-measure runs against it.** The 2018–2020 peak
  window shows 0.87/0.34/0.41 happiness claims per 1,000 messages vs 7.86 in
  late 2015 (dat:0024). Maximum intensity coincided with minimum reported
  happiness — the windfall funded the rate, not the reward.
- **The direction reversal.** The estate-money spine was corrected
  2026-08-18: the 2018 deep-cycle capital ran Dan-to-Suz (about $14,000 of
  ~$20,000 drawn down Aug–Oct 2018 transferred to her, $4,000 recovered
  before she stopped repaying), not toward Dan as the spine first claimed,
  and the $750/week figure was a single accusation generalised into a rate
  ([`dat:1293`](../data/1293-estate-spine-direction-reversal-dan-to-suz.md),
  [`dat:0679`](../data/0679-2018-deep-cycle-volume-and-correction.md)). The
  money was moving through relationships, being deployed, not arriving as
  rescue.

## The evidence against

- **No consumption ledger exists.** Every figure in the arc is retrospective
  testimony from one self-audit (dat:0062). No dated raw source for any
  figure was located; the corpus holds no purchase records, no dollar
  amounts for the 3.5–7g/day peak, no rate at all for the post-2020 band.
  The finance-as-independent-variable claim is an interpretation of an
  interpretation.
- **The September 2020 distribution is never_observed.** The threads end
  2020-08-26; whether the $134,069.31 arrived as described is unknown
  (dat:0051). The arc's ending — exhaustion vs investment — is literally
  the part the record doesn't cover.
- **Suz's itemised statement is unheld.** The 'Dan to Suzanne / $1000 /
  $14,000+ / Sum of transactions on app' statement (2026-08-18
  testimony, dat:1293) returns zero held-corpus hits for 'Dan to Suzanne'
  — the direction correction rests on the wiki's own re-read, and the
  $14,000 figure has no primary anchor here.

## How the ingest bears on it

The ingest did two things for this reading and one against it. **For:** the
six-advance sequence is now counterparty-sourced (attorney and executor
stating amounts in their own inbound words) with every numeric token
enumerated to rule out a seventh — this is the hardest money in the corpus
([`dat:0051`](../data/0051-estate-advances-both-pages-were-right.md)). **For:**
the spine's direction error was caught and corrected with dated blocks, so
the money's 2018 movement is at least honest about its direction. **Against:**
the ingest confirmed the dosage arc's figures have no primary source — the
one load-bearing quantitative claim about what the money bought remains
testimony.

## Open questions

- Did the September 2020 distribution arrive, and where did it go? This is
  the single largest open money question in the corpus.
- What were the actual 2017–2020 purchase amounts? Without them, "priced
  intensity" is a shape argument, not a ledger.
- How much of the six advances' $42,500 went to consumption vs the Suz
  deep-cycle vs ordinary life? The threads record requests confirmed, not
  money deployed.
