## The thesis

The wiki's unbroken-bond thesis — seventeen continuous years in the
pair-bond slot, the slot never voluntarily left empty — is second-order
synthesis over other pages' work, not a countable claim
[`dat:1209`](../data/1209-unbroken-bond-falsifier-and-recheck-machinery.md).
Its chronology is corroborated: Alexis Nov 2009–Nov 2015 (with a Jul–Oct
2013 separation), Annie Nov 2015–present, the single-month switch
(`dat:1205`). What the thesis lacks is a comparison — and the corpus
supplies two. This node argues: **Alexis is the control relationship and
Kristin is the negative case, and together they bound the "bond never
terminates" reading.** The slot can close. Not every ending resumes.

**The control: Alexis.** The relationship's opening is dated to the day —
Thanksgiving 2009 meeting, December 14 tweets (*"dan in love"*), the
January 23, 2010 tattoo eight weeks after they met
[`dat:0244`](../data/0244-alexis-armel-dating-and-commitment-speed.md).
The ending is small and accidental and, crucially, *terminal*: on February
19, 2025 Dan sent a message plainly intended for somebody else to a number
he no longer had straight — *"Guess what… Another 'New York I love you
but you're bringing me down' situation"* — she answered *"Who is this?"*,
he offered **WEXUS** as proof (a private household word from the 2011–2013
Gchat archive, fourteen years earlier), she replied *"Yup wrong number.
Sorry Dan."* Neither followed up
[`dat:0248`](../data/0248-alexis-armel-arrangement-and-last-message.md).
No resumption cycle. No 129 episodes. The same person who cannot terminate
a severance with Annie terminated this one with a wrong number and a
household password. The control proves the capacity exists; what differs
is the slot, not the man.

**The negative case: Kristin.** Ten weeks, Aug–Nov 2025 — "the only other
relationship of the Annie decade" — ended over $40, and the block has held
since December 2025. The terminal sixteen minutes verify message-for-message
in the held corpus: her *"Get off my shit I'll block you bitch"* (18:40
EST), his IP-address escalation, her *"All this is screenshotted. Blocking
you now. Don't contact me again"* (18:55), his *"mr. officer i live at 337
saratoga drive"* (18:56) — no charges documented on either side
[`dat:0803`](../data/0803-kristin-terminal-sixteen-minutes-corroborated.md).
The end-date revision (2025-09-01 → November, via the 2026-08-16 corpus
analysis, `dat:0304`) is the wiki correcting its own first/last-sighting
heuristic — the dating moved, the terminality didn't. A block held for
months, against the same person who resumes Annie contact in 36 seconds.
The negative case falsifies the universal version of "the bond never
terminates": endings that don't resume are in the record; they're just not
Annie endings.

## How the ingest's findings bear on it

The control's strength is its provenance chain. The WEXUS password is the
kind of detail only a kept archive produces: an unglossed household word
in the 2011–2013 Gchat archive becomes, fourteen years later, the password
on the relationship's last message — and the page earns it rather than
asserting it
[`dat:0248`](../data/0248-alexis-armel-arrangement-and-last-message.md).
The negative case's strength is the opposite: pure held-corpus
verification, beat for beat, with the clock translation (EST = UTC−5)
explicit
[`dat:0803`](../data/0803-kristin-terminal-sixteen-minutes-corroborated.md).

The ingest also files the honest limits. The $3,000 November 2018
arrangement — Dan offering Alexis as *"a plaything for Annie"* — shows the
control relationship was never cleanly linear even after the bond moved
(`dat:0248`); the control compares a post-bond entanglement's ending to a
bond's non-ending, and that asymmetry is the alternative this node must
carry. The Kristin end date is the wiki's reported conclusion from an
unheld 22,018-message analysis pass, not a re-derived fact (`dat:0304`) —
the terminality is corroborated, the exact dating is not.

## Contradictions

- The unbroken-bond page itself cites the July–October 2013 Alexis
  separation as corroborated rather than corrected by new operator
  testimony (`dat:1209`) — the bond thesis already *contains* a separation
  and reattachment inside the Alexis decade. The control case's "clean
  terminal ending" is the 2025 friendship's ending, not the 2015 bond's
  ending; the bond's own ending in 2015 was the switch to Annie, which is
  the thesis's central exhibit, not its counterexample.
- Kristin's block-holding is behaviorally identical to the current Annie
  block-holding (`int:block-as-first-held-boundary`). If the August 2026
  Annie severance holds to December 19, Kristin stops being the *exception*
  and becomes the *precedent* — the negative case would then be evidence
  that the capacity was there all along, and the control framing inverts.

## Open questions

- Whether the Alexis 2025 ending is truly terminal — the export's terminal
  message is per the page; absence of later contact is an absence claim
  over a corpus this pass doesn't exhaustively search.
- Whether "the only other relationship of the Annie decade" is a
  coverage claim or a finding — it rests on the wiki's completeness, and
  the 2020–2024 record hole constrains every claim about those years.
