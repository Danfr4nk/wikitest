## The thesis

The cognitive profile's failure mode is never ignorance. The forensic engine
works — Ti-dominance is categorical in the corpus (the narrow self-confidence
construction at 13 outbound / 0 inbound on the held slice,
[`dat:0890`](../data/0890-big-five-corpus-audit-and-self-typing.md)); the
calibrated-confidence page publishes its own single falsification rather than
hiding it. What does not happen is the step after diagnosis. The analysis is
the terminal product: produced at maximum resolution, acted on not at all.

## The instances

**The exit loop, fully diagnosed, fully repeated.** The Attachment Model's
RESOLVED recount moved severance declarations from 127/110 to 129 episodes
with 100% resumption and a 36-second median gap — the strongest quantitative
support the architecture ever had, and the page withdrew the weaker figure
rather than letting both stand
([`dat:0622`](../data/0622-attachment-model-129-episodes-resolution-withdraws-110-127.md)).
The analysis is perfect. The exits kept happening — 129 of them. Knowing the
36-second median did not make the 130th exit not happen.

**The reassurance architecture, mapped, not used.** The zero-phrase headline —
no "do you love me," no "are we ok," no "am i crazy," no "i cant do this"
across eleven years — replicates exactly in the held corpus
([`dat:0755`](../data/0755-reassurance-phrase-zeros-replicate-counts-diverge.md)).
He knows the structure of his own reassurance-seeking to the message. No
behavioral change is claimed downstream of the knowledge.

**The correction instinct, miscalibrated by its own calibration.** The wiki
recorded over-correcting a testimony date that was accurate — calibrated on
captures off by exactly one year, it carried that prior into a case where it
did not apply
([`dat:0017`](../data/0017-old-wiki-correction-instinct-overfired.md)). The
diagnostic system diagnosed its own error mechanism and named the transferable
lesson. The mechanism is the one this node is about: a narrow, correct finding
generalised past its evidence.

**The threat instrument, analyzed into uselessness.** The Morgantown campaign
ran 28 maternal-disclosure threats in 67 hours with one proven simulation —
after which Dan's own assertions of execution "can no longer serve as evidence
of execution" ([`dat:0257`](../data/0257-morgantown-call-leverage-campaign.md)).
The analysis is forensic and correct, and it terminates the instrument it
analyzes: the threats only worked while nobody counted them.

## The one interruption

The INTP profile page records "one documented interruption" of the Fe-inferior
pattern: the August 18, 2026 Ally "love bomb" exchange ending in "Okay that's
fair then" — verified verbatim
([`dat:0624`](../data/0624-conflict-architecture-love-bomb-exchange-primary-verified.md)).
One. In the entire record. The dossier treats it as load-bearing precisely
because it is the only instance of the diagnostic loop producing a behavioral
output. n=1 is not a pattern; it is a falsifier in waiting.

## The gap is fractal

It appears at the wiki level too. The system diagnosed corrections-don't-propagate
([`dat:0125`](../data/0125-end-fight-stale-attribution.md) names it; the
coordination note calls for a propagation pass) — and the diagnosis did not
propagate. The Context Core page ran a staleness audit on itself and the audit
went stale in the same way it describes. This is the shape
[`pat:reasoning-sound-provenance-unreliable`](../patterns/reasoning-sound-provenance-unreliable.md)
keeps describing: the reasoning is sound; what it produces does not travel.
