## Summary

Danielle "Dee" Onesi is Dan's first girlfriend (~2005–2009, Uniontown) and the
relationship the wiki's romantic line passes through on its way to Alexis: the
breakup was Dan's doing (infidelity at Full Sail), yet she never left the
picture — 17 years of continuous presence after the romance ended, ending up as
Suzanne Frank's closest friend and a Christmas fixture. She is the ex who
became family, and the corpus's most durable non-family female presence.
