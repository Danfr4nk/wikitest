## Summary

Alexis Armel is the six-year partner (Thanksgiving 2009 → November 2015) the
wiki treats as its control case: the long relationship that, in the page's own
thesis, "occupied the attachment slot without ever activating it," measured
against the eleven years with Annie that followed. The ingest leaves her the
best-dated relationship in the record — a tweet-anchored opening, a public-
register decay measured to the mention, and a 2020–2025 correspondence whose
last message was a wrong number. Her page also carries two unresolved
contradictions and a drug-chain account that rests on testimony alone.

## Evidence for the core claims

- **The opening is the record's most precisely dated relationship start.**
  An operator capture (2026-08-27) dated the Thanksgiving-2009 meeting; the
  tweet archive confirmed every load-bearing element (named "her" 29–30 Nov,
  first naming 14 Dec, "girlfriend" 16 Dec, Christmas together, return 30 Dec,
  tattoo evening of 23 Jan 2010). Testimony surviving contact with an
  independent artefact.
  [dat:0244](../data/0244-alexis-armel-dating-and-commitment-speed.md)
- **The public-register decay is measured, not asserted.** 85 rows naming her
  in the tweet archive; after 29 Nov 2013 a 149-day silence, two 2014
  mentions, then zero across twelve years. The affection register collapses in
  parallel: 12 of 32 mentions carry an affectionate token in 2010 against 3 of
  47 across 2011–2014. The page's falsification checks hold (he kept posting —
  171 posts in 2014; kept @-mentioning people — 57 distinct handles, an
  all-time high; the 2013 move home preceded the mention *peak*, not the
  silence).
  [dat:0245](../data/0245-alexis-armel-public-register-arithmetic.md)
- **The $3,000 transactional origin is corpus-verified.** *'Initially I offered
  her $3000 to be a plaything for Annie'* (2018-11-28, Dan, held corpus); the
  reunion's quoted beats all verify verbatim except one genuine misquote
  ("THE COOL LIGHTS IN HERE", not "THERE").
  [dat:1067](../data/1067-alexis-3000-plaything-offer-2018-11-28.md),

  [dat:0060](../data/0060-alexis-thanksgiving-2009-final-pre-stabilization-episode.md),
  [dat:0244](../data/0244-alexis-armel-dating-and-commitment-speed.md)
- **The hidden-camera/orchestration telling is page testimony.** The
  orchestration-and-voyeurism page's quotes (hidden-camera request, declining
  to participate, leaving for drug logistics, the "going to make me religious"
  line) do not appear in the held 192,140-row corpus — carried as prior-wiki
  testimony with no independent held corroboration.
  [dat:1073](../data/1073-alexis-reunion-hidden-camera-orchestration.md)
- **The Shumar arrest detail is relayed, not held.** The granular Feb 14–16,
  2017 account (fifteen staged officers, $35,000 bail, the seized rig, the plea,
  SCI Muncy) comes through the old wiki's transcription of the Lucas Thomas
  Facebook thread; raw/facebook-threads/ holds only the manifest. The one
  correction made against held data: the thread is 118 messages (63 from Dan),
  not the page's stale ~45.
  [dat:0247](../data/0247-alexis-armel-shumar-arrest-2017.md)
- **The "control case" thesis is the wiki's reading, not a measurement.** Six
  years that "occupied the attachment slot without ever activating it" is the
  page's interpretive frame — it stands on the tweet decay and the dormancy
  shape, not on any independent instrument.

## Contradictions found by the ingest

- **Two differently-dated eviction-and-concealment accounts, held side by side.**
  Account A (2026-08-27 capture): March 2014 eviction after the washing-machine
  incident, five months of concealment, then a Leah Tedesco residence, by
  Christmas 2014 at 155 Virginia Ave. Account B (older): four months hidden,
  placed immediately before the January 2015 lease and the February 2015
  possession arrest. The page holds both without reconciling — correctly, since
  both are operator testimony of different specificity rather than primary
  record against testimony.
  [dat:0246](../data/0246-alexis-armel-hidden-year-contradiction.md)
- **The tattoo timing was corrected on the page itself:** first read as "four
  months in," corrected 2026-09-02 to eight weeks — the corrected figure is the
  tweet-verified one.
