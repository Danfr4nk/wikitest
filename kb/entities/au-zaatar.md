## Summary

Au Za'atar was Dan's NYC restaurant employer, **March 2021 – August
2024 — 41 months** (arithmetic checks: Mar 2021 → Aug 2024 = 41).
The page calls it his longest job and his most-visited GPS location
(445 visits) — but see the Nemacolin contradiction below. Hired as a
busser with zero food-service experience, ended with the August 2024
shed shutdown, ruled **involuntary** (DanAnnie dossier Amendment 2,
ACCEPTED). The tenure's texture rests almost entirely on unheld
sources; the held corpus is nearly silent on the restaurant.

## The tenure

- **Hired March 2021** as a busser, zero food-service experience; talked
  into the veterans' equal tip split on day one (page testimony).
- **Location:** the Midtown restaurant (58th & 1st) — **REVISED
  [2026-07-13]**: not the East Village flagship as the page originally
  said. Annie worked as hostess at the original East Village location;
  Dan got her the job ~April 2021, and her zero-notice firing was from
  that separate location (page testimony, [`dat:0452`](../data/0452-au-zaatar-employment-block-41-months.md)).
- **The Shed:** service out of a plywood COVID-era "Shed" on the 58th
  Street sidewalk, rebuilt nightly (2–3 hours), which Dan turned into
  the "Outside Guy" post with sole custody of the shed's cash tips —
  and which he never closed once in 41 months (page testimony).
- **Pay**, confirmed by Dan directly 2026-07-14: **$15/hour base plus
  tip pool, weekly Sunday checks typically $800–1,000 for a six-day
  week** (page testimony).
- **End:** the August 2024 shed shutdown. The loss is ruled involuntary
  (dossier Amendment 2, ACCEPTED): hours cut Jun–Aug 2024, resumes from
  July 9, unemployment filed August 8. Contemporaneous first-person:
  *"By the way they officially let me go from AZ"* (2024-08-21, held
  corpus, local date — see the timezone note in
  [`dat:0443`](../data/0443-dimitri-thread-held-corpus-71-job-loss-aug-21-local.md));
  *"Tarik was really really nice about it and everything though. We
  parted on good terms"*; *"And I am already getting unemployment so it
