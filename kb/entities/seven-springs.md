## Summary

Seven Springs Mountain Resort (~25 miles from Uniontown, PA) was Dan's
principal adolescent social world — every weekend, stronger than his
high-school friendships — built around a family ski-in-ski-out condo,
**unit K2** in the Villages at Seven Springs (Sunridge development).
The resort's terrain-park build-out (2000→2006) happened inside exactly
the window Dan was living it; his first cocaine use is dated by his own
account to **2004**, in the ski-crew context — a full year before the
November 2005 hinge event the rest of the corpus treats as the inciting
rupture.

## The condo

**Unit K2, the Villages at Seven Springs, ski-in-ski-out** — the
strongest claim on the page, because two independently written 2026
captures converge on it: the 2026-08-01 bald-eagle-cummings capture
behind [`dat:0209`](../data/0209-bald-eagle-cummings.md) (records the
family renting at Seven Springs for years before buying unit K2 in the
Sunridge development) and the 2026-08-09 extreme-sports capture behind
[`dat:0107`](../data/0107-seven-springs-k2-condo-and-park-infrastructure.md).
Two separate 2026 operator captures agreeing on K2 is the best
corroboration anything on this page has.

## The park build-out (single-source)

Per the 2026-08-09 extreme-sports capture (dat:0107): across Dan's
skiing years the resort's park infrastructure grew from **one
north-face park** — 12-foot halfpipe with a bad run-in, a rope tow, a
slopestyle section with 3–4 features (two small jumps, two real park
rails) — to, by the 2006 season, the **"premiere terrain park of the
mid-Atlantic"** with a full dedicated slopestyle run of several dozen
features — credited to the **Nuttings' takeover** (Olympic-sized pipe
cutter, real park crews, money). The park-inventory numbers and the
Nuttings causal claim have **no second source**: precise enough to be
checkable against the resort's own development history, which nobody
here has done. Filed as unverified with the reason stated — operator
recall, not resort records.

## The 4Bi9 thread

The capture's 4Bi9 thread — Tom Wallisch showing up at Seven Springs a
few times a year pre-fame, preferring the Roundtop/Liberty scene
culture that became **4Bi9 Media** — is consistent with the public
record: Wallisch was born in Pittsburgh, night-skied Seven Springs as a
schoolkid, and broke out via 2007 videos produced by 4Bi9 Media
(dat:0107; web check 2026-09-09: Powder, Wikipedia, a Wallisch bio PDF).
Dan researched Wallisch at length as an adult **without knowing him
personally** — retrospective interest, not friendship
(dat:1297).

## The 2004 dating and what it complicates

Per Dan's own retrospective account (2026-08-09 capture,
[`dat:1393`](../data/1393-seven-springs-first-exposure.md)): the ski
crew was his first drug-exposure cohort, and his **first cocaine use is
dated to 2004**, in that crew's context — a full year before the
November 2005 hinge (father's rehab disclosure + parents' divorce) the
rest of the corpus treats as the inciting rupture. A later self-audit
frames the first use as **instrumental** (a weight-control tool tied to
adolescent bulimia) as much as social. That complicates a clean "the
divorce caused it" story — worth stating plainly, because the hinge
narrative depends on the clean version. Note the page's own internal
tension: its connection block lists both 2004 and age 17–18 (2005–2006)
framings for first cocaine use; filed as the page states it, with the
2004/ski-crew dating as the headline claim.

The same year, 2004, he began dating Danielle Onesi — his first
documented girlfriend.
