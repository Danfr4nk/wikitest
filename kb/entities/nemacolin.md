## Summary

Nemacolin Woodlands Resort (Farmington, PA) was Dan's employer as a golf
caddy, **April 2016 – November 2019** — a resume-derived 43-month span
("Experience Associate – Golf"), worked alongside Laurel Valley and
Pikewood National day trips. The caddying years were his main income
through his hardest financial stretch, overlapping the Fran vigil,
the Suboxone years, and the 2018–2020 Uniontown supply-network era. The
page splits cleanly: held-corroborated beats vs unheld resume/dump
narrative ([`dat:0419`](../data/0419-nemacolin-caddying-held-corroborations.md),
[`dat:0420`](../data/0420-nemacolin-caddying-unheld-details.md)).
