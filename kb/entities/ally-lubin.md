## Summary

Alexandra Lubin, b. June 26, 1990 — remote STEM professional in Charlotte,
NC; the eighteen-year attachment Dan has never met in person. The one tie
that has never been a cohabitation or an arrangement: conducted almost
entirely remotely, running concurrently with the Annie relationship the
whole time. In August 2026 she became the first person outside Dan ever to
read the wiki. As of the corpus end: live romantic fixation and low-drama
friendship, with trust she says is not yet earned and concrete life
instructions she issues — stop isolating, text his father Rick.

## The eighteen years, dated

The "18-year" framing is Dan's and it has a checkable base: both of them
independently locate her peak in **2008**. She: 2026-08-18, *"I peaked in
2008."* He: writing to Dan Polyak on **December 7, 2018**, hours after his
first real exchange with her — *"i've spent 10 years idolizing this girl
i've never met as the epitome of whatever 'cool' is"* — ten years back
puts the start at 2008. She experiences 2008 as loss (*"And I'll never be
this young again"*); he as the thing he has been pursuing since. Note the
one-year wobble the entity page itself flags: his 2026-08-21 love letter
says "17 years and counting," which lands in 2009 — unresolved absent a
third date
([`dat:0211`](../data/0211-ally-lubin-both-locate-peak-2008.md)).
