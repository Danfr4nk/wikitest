## Summary

Rebecca Diane Van Voorhis ("Diane Moore"), b. 1939 — Dan's maternal
grandmother, **Fran Coldren's only child**, and the hostile pole of the
maternal line. She served Dan the 2018 letter barring him from Fran's house
while Fran was dying; Dan believes the letter was drafted by her second
husband Dave Moore, who acted as her proxy to hospital social workers
during the vigil. Adversarial and arm's-length: the one family member whose
posture toward Dan is documented hostility rather than distance.

## The exclusion letter, corrected

One letter, not two; served late, not early. A letter reached Dan from
Diane setting rules for his presence in Fran's house, demanding the keys,
and closing with *"any violation of the rules will be considered
trespassing."* The page's 2026-08-18 correction, adjudicated against Dan's
contemporaneous 2018-04-03 messages:

- **One letter, addressed to him** — *"lol also if they are writing such
  professional correspondence why wouldn't Annie get her own letter"* —
  Annie was upset about that one.
- **The eviction notice was served 2018-03-29** and the letter was being
  read, forwarded and lawyered on **2018-04-03, the day before Fran died**
  — not "well before." The "well before" framing came from a 2026 capture;
  where a 2026 recollection and a 2018 message disagree about 2018, the
  message governs.
- Dan's own read on the night of April 3: *"i think dave wrote it"* —
  **Dave Moore, Diane's second husband**, who two days earlier had been
  "speaking on her behalf" to the hospital's social workers while she was
  unreachable. And "Dian V. Moore," the server of the March 29 eviction
  notice, is Diane herself (gap closed 2026-08-18)
  ([`dat:0172`](../data/0172-diane-exclusion-letter-correction.md)).

Its operative effect: the two people who had held the house together for
three years were given **no shifts** in the Fred Adams–mandated rotation;
they attended anyway, off the roster, and were the two in the room at the
end ([`dat:0172`](../data/0172-diane-exclusion-letter-correction.md)).

  rupture; its post-April-2018 tail is nearly empty — one accidental
  contact 2018-08-01, one hostility outburst 2020-03-28, and the
  coronavirus-era message. No reconciliation is on record.
