## Summary

Frances Coldren (née Jesse Frances Thomas), 1920-08-15 – 2018-04-04 —
Dan's maternal great-grandmother, who raised him alongside Suz and is named
by both Dan and Suz as the guiding force of their lives. She is the biggest
life influence in his record; her death is the corpus's most documented
grief event. Dan and [Annie Ulmer](annie-ulmer.md) were her paid
caregivers in 2017–18. The Numark NS7 she gifted him is one of his life
anchors. During the April 2018 death vigil, her daughter
[Diane Moore](diane-moore.md) barred Dan from Fran's house.

## Life, as the genealogy carries it

Genealogy (read from the unheld 23andMe GEDCOM zip — attributed testimony,
filed honestly): born **Jesse Frances Thomas**, Fort Martin WV,
1920-08-15, one of ten recorded children of Marion Edward Thomas Sr. (b.
1877) and Ida Ellen Conwell (b. 1880). Three marriages — **Emmet Graden Van
Voorhis** (b. 1917; issue Rebecca Diane, b. 1939), **Thomas Whyel** (b.
~1897, coal and coke; no issue), **Ira Burdette Coldren Jr.** (b. 1924,
lawyer, West Point, 33rd-degree Mason; no issue). Name change Jesse →
Frances at about eighteen; middle initial T taken on marrying Ira — the
family's account says it memorialises Thomas Whyel, though Thomas was also
her own maiden surname, so both readings stand. Suz carries Whyel as a
middle name. The April 1940 census places Emmet, Fran and infant Diane in
Smithfield, Fayette County; **117 Belmont Circle tenure from ~1961 — 57
years, not the round 50** the page had carried.

Athletic record (newspaper clippings and family captures, unheld): first
hole-in-one 1959 at LaGorce Country Club (Miami Beach); second 1969 at
Uniontown Country Club, 153-yard 11th with a No. 5 wood; UCC Women's Golf
Crown at 342 over 72 holes, won by seven strokes; a 113-pound tarpon boated
at the Met Tournament in the Everglades; commemorative pin for a 200 game
at Manor Lanes; photographs beside Arnold Palmer's private plane.

Gift protocol (operator captures, unheld): **$100 per year of the
recipient's life**, from about age 13. The Numark NS7 II was bought on top
of the annual cheque and — per the 2026 capture — was **not spontaneous**:
Dan convinced her to buy it
([`dat:0447`](../data/0447-fran-coldren-gedcom-golf-gift-protocol-unverifiable-rows.md)).

## The vigil (March–April 2018)

- The **second fall, "Down Goes Frazier," came the night of March 7–8,
  2018** — Dan to Vicki, verified in the held corpus: *"she was screaming
  so loud when they were putting her on the gurney it was just awful"*
  (2018-03-08 18:29:31 UTC / 13:29 EST)
  ([`dat:0169`](../data/0169-fran-vigil-fall-and-hospital-sequence.md)).
- From the hospital she was mandated to a nursing home — the outcome her
  stay-at-home directive was built to prevent; within ~two weeks her state
  failed completely.
- **March 29, mid-collapse:** Dan was served an eviction notice terminating
  his residency at 155 Virginia Ave and demanding the keys — served by
  "Dian V. Moore," identified 2026-08-18 as [Diane Moore](diane-moore.md)
  herself ([`dat:0169`](../data/0169-fran-vigil-fall-and-hospital-sequence.md)).
- **April 1, 2018** (first day of caddie season): Fran was moved to the
  Uniontown Hospital pediatric wing to die; **Judge Fred Adams — Ira's old
  law partner, trustee of the estate accounts — mandated a 24-hour
  rotation**, held in shifts by Dan, Annie, Vicki, and Marla.
- During the vigil Dan set off a hospital-wide fire alarm with a sub-ohm
  vape: **four fire trucks, a security escort off the property, charging
  documents** — Fayette summary non-traffic docket
  **MJ-14101-NT-0000082-2018, filed April 5, 2018**, single local-ordinance
  count of smoking in a hospital. The positions self-contradict in Dan's own
  accounts (vigil page: Dan blowing clouds in the room, Suz in the bathroom;
  two later captures invert it) — the old wiki's veracity ledger carries it
  as self_contradicted, neither account contemporaneous
  ([`dat:0171`](../data/0171-fran-vigil-vape-alarm-summons.md)).

## The death: April 4, 2018

**Fran died April 4, 2018, with Dan and Annie the two in the room at the
moment itself.** Dan's contemporaneous verdict: *"fran was happy when she
died SPECIFICALLY because annie and i were there with her."* Three minutes
later it began to snow outside Uniontown Hospital. The April 1 vs April 4
contradiction is resolved: **April 1 was the admission; April 4 was the
death** — DANSYNTH's April 1 death date conflated the two. The April 4 date
is fixed by two independent dated messages, both verified in the held
iMessage corpus: Marla's *"She made it thru the nite"* at 2018-04-04
11:23:49 UTC (07:23 EST) — the last one — and Davey Fitzpatrick's
unprompted *"Sorry for your loss bro"* at 2018-04-05 18:47:29 UTC
(14:47 EST). A memorial celebration followed at the Uniontown Country Club,
**May 6, 2018, 12–4 PM**
([`dat:0170`](../data/0170-fran-death-date-april-4-2018.md)).

The death-moment story and its retraction are both verified verbatim with
exact timestamps: the story (mental "give me some kind of sign" request,
the blink, the smile, the slow passing) at 2018-04-06 00:10:05 UTC (= Apr
5 20:10 EDT); the retraction — *"upon further review of the video, it
looks like a monitor went off alerting us that her pulse was dropping...
def isn't as 'supernatural' knowing that fact"* — at 2018-04-06 03:26:52
UTC (= Apr 5 23:26 EDT), roughly three hours later, verified to the minute.
The retraction came unprompted; the page kept both
([`dat:0446`](../data/0446-fran-coldren-evidence-table-and-death-moment-retraction.md)).

Dan's fullest first-person account of the death went to **Lucie Dobbin**,
Fran's Florida-winters friend, on the evening of April 5 — the silent
mental-communication attempt, the final smile, the immediate pronouncement
on Dan's phone recording. The thread bodies are unheld (Facebook contents
not in this repository), filed as testimony at low confidence
([`dat:0761`](../data/0761-lucie-dobbin-fran-death-night-testimony.md)).

## The caregiving, in Dan's own words

*"she was a trip dude but i spent the past 6 months looking after her full
time ($15 an hour to play video games with gram is a good deal) and we had
a lot of fun. got good closure and finally did something in my life that
wasn't completely selfish haha."* — 2018-04-06 21:55:47 UTC (= 17:55 EDT),
four days after the death, unprompted, to a third party; held-corpus
verbatim. The six months and $15/hour are his contemporaneous statement,
not the wiki's inference — cited against the multi-year caregiving span
the old wiki had inferred
([`dat:0708`](../data/0708-acquisition-drive-fran-vigil-message-verified.md)).

Inheritance anticipation ran alongside the grief: *"my inheritance doesn't
get distributed until we liquidate all her assets"* (Apr 7); *"i got my
inheritance check from grandma today"* (Apr 27 19:59 EDT)
([`dat:0446`](../data/0446-fran-coldren-evidence-table-and-death-moment-retraction.md)).
And his last word to her, two days after she died: *"thanks buddy. gram
did aaaaaanything i asked her to, so the least i could do was be there for
her"* (2018-04-06 19:22 EDT)
([`dat:0447`](../data/0447-fran-coldren-gedcom-golf-gift-protocol-unverifiable-rows.md)).
