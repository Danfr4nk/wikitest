## Summary

Gabe was Dan's cat — solid black, long-haired — adopted in Orlando in
August 2008 (picked out by Danielle Onesi the day after Dan arrived for
Full Sail) and euthanized **2023-10-30** (Dan's own contemporaneous
message, *"I had to put gabe down today"*; corpus-verified in
[`dat:0292`](../data/0292-gabe-death-2023-10-30-corrects-memory.md)).
Fifteen years with the household, five relocations, named for Gabe
Saporta of Cobra Starship as one leg of Dan's deliberate
provocative-naming set ("Milo, Gabe, Max").

entity_kind is "other": the schema enum has no animal kind; see
[`ent:milo`](milo.md) for the same choice and reasoning.

## Adoption

August 2008, Orlando: Danielle Onesi picked him out the day after Dan
arrived at Full Sail ([`dat:0487`](../data/0487-danielle-onesi-first-girlfriend-gabe-james.md)).
The page adds the shelter texture ([`dat:0534`](../data/0534-gabe-adoption-naming-relocation-new-detail.md)):
Danielle chose him because he was pawing at the cats in neighboring
cages, and his paperwork noted he got along well with rabbits — *which
the two of them found endearing*. He was Dan's first solid-black cat and
his first long-haired cat. Danielle is the figure who never left the
picture — Suz's closest friend, a Christmas fixture — and she is the one
who paid for Gabe's euthanasia in 2023 when Dan couldn't afford it: the
Gemini analysis's named concept **"Cat Debt"** (dat:0487).

## Relocation chain

Orlando → NYC → PA → PA again → NYC again — five legs across fifteen
years (old-wiki gabe page, per dat:0534; the NYC eras in the standing
record are 2010–13 and 2019–25). The chain is page testimony, unheld in
this repository.

## Naming

Dan's own articulated naming logic, quoted verbatim from an unheld AI
session (`raw/self/dox-md/Max.md`, via dat:0534): *"my cat gabe was
named for douchebag cobra starship singer and fucking rad midtown
singer Gabe Saporta"* — Dan's stated logic is that he picked the name
*for the pivot itself* (Midtown's "fucking rad" era to Cobra Starship's
"douchebag" turn), one leg of a three-name set ("Milo, Gabe, Max") drawn
from people he calls *"the most reprehensible people society is throwing
up,"* used as a social tripwire/diagnostic. This is a stated doctrine,
not an observed pattern — Dan's own motive quote, not the wiki's gloss —
and it matches the standing memory note (provocative namings deliberate;
he reads strangers' reactions to the name).

## Death

**2023-10-30.** Dan's message (is_from_me=1) of 2023-10-30 21:45:44 UTC
(= 17:45 EDT, local date unambiguous): *"I had to put gabe down today"*;
a second send of the same text follows 2023-10-31 00:12:28 UTC. The
master timeline's Tier-1 entry is confirmed by Eric Jester's independent
same-date message (dat:0292; the Jester message itself was not
re-verified in that pass).

Two page errors the dated record corrects:
- The page prints the euthanasia date as **"November 2003"** — "almost
  certainly a typo for November 2023 … flagged here pending
  confirmation." The contemporaneous corpus record settles it at
  2023-10-30, so the pending confirmation is closed (dat:0534).
- The page's phrasing — "shortly after his own birthday" — is backwards:
  October 30 is **two days before** Dan's November 1 birthday, not after
  it.
- The standing memory note's "d. Nov 2023" is imprecise against the
  dated record: October 30, 2023.

## The MAX_PRIME contradiction

The 2026-era bootloader (MAX_PRIME.md) refers to Gabe in the present
tense — *"the food and the cat are always real"*; *"the Milo-style
updates about Gabe are not deflection from the real stuff"* — for a cat
dead since October 2023 (dat:0534). Two horns: stale bootloader text, or
the date is wrong. The contemporaneous first-person *"I had to put gabe
down today"* outranks a bootloader's tense, so the likelier reading is
that the bootloader text is older than its last-edited date. The date
side of the contradiction is closed; the bootloader's provenance stays
open.
