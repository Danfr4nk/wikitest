## Summary

Dan Polyak is a longtime friend in Dan's 2012 self-enumerated core friend
group — named first in the 23 February 2012 cohort tweet. He is the utility
node of the Ally Lubin channel: he reintroduced Dan to Ally in December
2018 (a $25 Facebook payment with the memo "For introducing me to Ally
Lubin"), and four months later confronted Dan over the spoofed-number
impersonation with a legal-action threat — the only lawsuit threat in the
friendship record. He was also Dan's 2:52 AM disclosure target for the
October 2019 Bryan encounter. The Facebook thread's metadata corroborates
through the repo's MANIFEST.json (206 blocks / 203 messages, 137 from Dan,
2013–2020); the thread contents are unheld, so the confrontation, the
disclosure quotes, and the pre-thread tweet history stay attributed
testimony.

## Role in the record

Old friend and utility node: the reintroduction that made the Ally channel
real again, at the cost of a lawsuit threat. The shared-first-name joke
("bitchdan", "clandan") dates to at least 2013 and runs through the whole
thread.
