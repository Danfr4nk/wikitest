## Summary

Full Sail University (Orlando) is where Dan studied Recording Arts and,
per his own 2026-09-09 correction, **graduated in September 2009** —
not August (his misremembering) and not December (what the record
appeared to show). The post-graduation months were audit, labs and Pro
Tools certification work, not continued enrollment. The Pro Tools 210M
certification is dated to the minute in platform-timestamped tweets
(January 26, 2010); the Thanksgiving-2009 Roxicet account is 2026
testimony with no contemporaneous corroboration. The old-wiki Full Sail
page is stale against the correction and needs regeneration.

## The September resolution

The operator stated to this session on 2026-09-09, unprompted by a
question about the December evidence and as a correction to the
published page: he graduated Full Sail in **September 2009**, not
December, and after graduating he audited one class, continued doing
labs, and was still working toward his Pro Tools operator certification
([`dat:0058`](../data/0058-graduation-september-2009-then-audit-and-certification.md)).
That single account makes all four dated artefacts true at once — it is
not a fourth hypothesis competing with the other three.

The prior wiki's testimony ledger had already scored *"graduated Full
Sail (AS Recording Arts) in August 2009"* as REFUTED — September is not
August — but on the wrong grounds (it refuted it by reading him as
enrolled until December). Right answer, wrong route.

**Still unheld:** no transcript, diploma or certification record is in
this repository. A Full Sail transcript would move the account from
testimony to first-party; Avid publishes operator certifications, and
a dated credential would independently fix the December work as
certification work.

out **February 16, 2010** with Suboxone induction ("day zero") on

The page is explicit about the gap: its "What the tweets do not
corroborate" section names the Roxicet and Spetch among the details with
no contemporaneous support (the held message corpus's real-text rows
begin November 2015, so no 2009 message could corroborate anyway). Filed
at the testimony tier: detailed, dated, internally consistent, no
primary-source anchor. The February 17, 2010 day-zero date is the
chemical-history record's standing account of the Suboxone start.
