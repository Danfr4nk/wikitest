## Summary

Ismaila Barry ("DJ") is a Senegalese busser-turned-server at Au Za'atar who
made the same busser-to-server climb Dan did — the parallel climb on which
their friendship was built. The record counts him among Dan's best friends,
one of the few relationships the corpus ranks that high. Where Dimitri is the
loved boss and Tarik the anchor, DJ is the peer: the friendship the floor
produced.
