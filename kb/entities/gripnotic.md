## Summary

**GRIPNOTIC** is Dan's current primary music brand — bass house / tech
house / DnB-adjacent, defined as **remixes and AI voice drops**
(instrumental and spoken-word, never sung original lyrics — a
thirteen-year pattern across all four aliases, page testimony). The
held corpus contains **exactly three** mentions of the name, all
landing within ~7.5 hours on **2026-04-06** (Eastern) — the
music-reactivation moment the page names — and the count replicates
exactly ([`dat:1380`](../data/1380-gripnotic-alias-corpus-mentions.md),
[`dat:1025`](../data/1025-alias-dates-held-checks.md)).

## The studio correction

The old wiki/interests/music/overview's corpus search claimed "one
Dan-sent message in fifteen years about making a track, **zero about a
studio**." The held slice does not reproduce the zero: **15 Dan-sent
messages** contain 'studio' (2015-12-11 → 2026-04-20). All 15 are,
however, gear/room/AI-shaped — closet-studio photos (2015-12-11,
2015-12-15), studio monitors for a "booth mix" (2017-04-22), a
Full-Sail-era "final bounce of our studio project" (2017-05-08), a room
"turned into a studio" (2018-10-23), seven Google/AI/Firebase 'studio'
dev links (2024–2026) — **never a session booked, a studio visited, or
music being made as current activity** (dat:1025). The literal claim is
false; the thesis it carried survives corrected: production talk in
eleven years of held messages is exclusively home-gear, schoolwork, or
AI tooling — the production identity is held as self-concept, almost
unmentioned as activity.
