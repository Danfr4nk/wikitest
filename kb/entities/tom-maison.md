## Summary

Tom Maison is Dan's primary male friend — "the clearest safe lateral (peer,
non-paternal) attachment in the corpus" — from the adolescent Seven Springs
scene onward: crisis anchor, 2025–26 supply line, recurring participant in the
arrangement, and the man who introduced Dan to Kristin Prentiss in August
2025. The ingest corrects his corpus weight downward ~2.9x (Kristin's handle
was mislabeled under his), resolves the MAX_PRIME identity question by merging
the drug-supply contact into the friend (as page-attributed synthesis), leaves
the 2025-10-11 traffic stop unverified in the held corpus, and flags the
"standing supply line" framing against a 2025 testimony reversal that may
recast him as failover, not default.
