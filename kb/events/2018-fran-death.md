## What happened

Fran Coldren — Jesse Frances Thomas, born 1920-08-15 in Fort Martin, WV,
Dan's maternal great-grandmother, named by him as the biggest influence
of his life — died **April 4, 2018** at Uniontown Hospital, with Dan and
Annie the two in the room. Dan's contemporaneous verdict: *"fran was
happy when she died SPECIFICALLY because annie and i were there with
her."* Three minutes later it began to snow outside the hospital
(`1080p.mov`). It is the most documented grief event in the corpus.

## Resolved contradictions (kept on record)

- **April 1 vs April 4.** Resolved: April 1 was the pediatric-wing
  admission, April 4 the death. DANSYNTH's April 1 death date conflated
  the two. The April 4 date is fixed by two independent held-corpus
  messages (Marla's last "made it thru the nite" the morning of the 4th;
  Davey's condolence the 5th). [dat:0170]
- **2017 vs 2018.** Two 2026 captures date the fall-and-vigil sequence
  to March–April 2017; seven independent records date it to 2018
  (GEDCOM death 2018-04-04, Marla thread, Vicki gurney messages, the
  Danny thread opening 2018-02-16, the eviction notice, Davey's
  condolence, Ellen Ulmer's April 2018 condolence). The page's inference
  for the misdating mechanism: April 2017 and April 2018 shared the
  caddie-season-opening tag, and April 2017 held a second death Dan
  processed that week (Jay Lauer, overdose, April 11, 2017) — a true
  2017 death fused onto the 2018 one. [dat:0494]
- **"Down Goes Frazier"** is the corpus's contemporaneous name for the
  fall — the record's own idiom, not the wiki's. [dat:0494]

## The death-moment story and its retraction, both verified

This is the ingest's most-quoted passage, and both halves survive intact
in the held corpus:

- **The story** (2018-04-06 00:10:05 UTC = Apr 5 20:10 EDT): the silent
  request for a sign, the blink "for the first time in a week," the
  smile, the slow passing.
- **The retraction** (2018-04-06 03:26:52 UTC = Apr 5 23:26 EDT):
  reviewing the video shows a monitor went off alerting them that her
  pulse was dropping — *"def isn't as 'supernatural' knowing that fact.
  but doesn't take away from the cool moment we had with gram."*

The three-hour gap, the minute-exact Eastern conversions, and the
page's 2026-08-02 rebuild methodology (age 97 settled, $15/hr and
six-months established, surgery and nursing-home dates recovered) all
check out — the strongest single-page corroboration in the ingest.
[dat:0446]

## Open questions

- **The February 16 vs March 7–8 fall mechanics.** The held-corpus spine
  is the March 7–8 "Down Goes Frazier" fall (gurney quote, next-day
  timestamp). The 2026-capture reconstruction puts a screaming fall,
  a 911 call, and a broken hip on February 16 as well — sharing enough
  detail with the March account (screaming, ambulance, gurney) that
  fusion in the 2026 retelling cannot be ruled out. Against that: the
  March 9 surgery message reads as though the hospital course is
  beginning, not continuing. The night is the arrangement's one
  corpus-verified fact (the Danny thread); the falls' choreography is
  attributed testimony. This tension is filed, not resolved.
- **The vape-alarm summons** (MJ-14101-NT-0000082-2018, filed 2018-04-05):
  disposition unknown; the legal index carries nothing the vigil page
  lacked. [dat:0495-uniontown-hospital-vape-alarm](../data/0495-uniontown-hospital-vape-alarm.md)
- Whether the March 29 eviction notice, the April 3 letter, and any
  earlier visiting-rules letter are one document, two, or three.
  [dat:0172]
- What Danny Matthews knew (his thread continues with no reference to
  the fall night). [dat:0494]
