## What happened

Dan graduated Full Sail University in Winter Park, Florida, with a
Recording Arts degree in **September 2009** — top 5% of his class, Pro
Tools HD certified — then stayed on through December auditing one class,
running labs, and finishing his Pro Tools operator certification. The
September date is Dan's own correction to this wiki, made 2026-09-09 when
he read the published site and found the month wrong.

## Why the September date is solid

Four dated artefacts sat on the record with no reading that took all four:
the August 31 tweet pointing at September, the September 26 "just
graduated today" said twice in one day, the November–December
coursework-looking tweets, and the old wiki's "graduated August 2009" —
which matched none of them. The operator's 2026-09-09 correction is not a
fourth competing hypothesis; it is the one state of the world in which all
four artefacts are true at once. A graduate can sit in a lab, take a
practical, and sit an AWS final — and would write a tweet about it exactly
as if still enrolled.

The correction arrived because the wiki was published and browsable and
its subject read it and found a claim about his own life that was wrong —
the first time that loop has ever closed. [dat:0058] treats testimony that
*explains residue* as a different, stronger instrument than testimony that
merely asserts a fact.

## What this corrects here

- **The prior wiki's "August 2009" is stale.** Its testimony ledger
  scored t013 (*"graduated Full Sail (AS Recording Arts) in August 2009"*,
  stated certain) as REFUTED. September is not August.
- **The prior wiki's own reason for the refutation does not survive.**
  It refuted August on the grounds that he was in school until December;
  the correct grounds are that he finished in September — one month
  later than he remembered, not four months earlier than the record
  appeared to show. Right answer, wrong route. [dat:0058]
- The published wikitest framing of *"still enrolled in December"* and of
  September-vs-December being *"in tension"* is superseded: the tension
  was an artefact of assuming coursework implies degree enrolment.
- The 2026-08-27 Alexis capture dates the meeting to "a few months after
  the August 2009 graduation" — the month is stale; the sequencing holds.
  [dat:0244-alexis-armel-dating-and-commitment-speed]

## Evidence against / limits

- **No transcript, diploma, or certification record is held.** This is
  Dan's account of his own education, filed as testimony. A Full Sail
  transcript would move it from attributed testimony to first-party. Avid
  publishes operator certifications; a dated credential would
  independently fix the December work as certification rather than
  coursework.
- The tweet quotations are the prior wiki's transcriptions; the tweet
  archive is not held here. The Facebook messages were read directly.
- Top-5%-of-class is from the longstanding canonical record, not from a
  held primary source.
