## What happened

The eleven-year relationship with Annie Ulmer began in the last week of
November 2015, overlapping the eviction of Alexis Armel to the day. The
corpus's first rows are dated November 28–29; the relationship predates
the record (both speakers treat "day one" as past tense), and the
November 29 Uniontown Country Club meeting is a **first in-person
meeting, not a first contact**. The merger idiom is present in the first
hour: *"YOU ARE MY EVERYTHING"* went out at 03:24 EST, roughly ninety
minutes after they met in person.

This is the corpus's central thesis — the "bond switch" — and the
largest single relationship record in Dan's archive: 97,768 unique
messages over the decade.

## The [when] block, defended

The hard-dated spine runs **2015-11-28 to 2015-12-01**. The `start` of
2015-11-24 is inference: Annie's December 1 message dates the
introduction one week back, and "day one" is already in the past tense
on the corpus's first day. No message names the introduction itself, so
the 24th is a one-day-precision estimate from a one-week message, and it
is marked as such rather than asserted flatly.

## Evidence for

- **The minute-level corroboration is the rare kind.** The page dated
  "YOU ARE MY EVERYTHING" to 03:24 EST from a read pass; the held corpus
  carries the same text at 08:24:14 UTC = 03:24 EST, an exact match.
  Page and corpus agree to the minute with no displacement.
  [dat:0165-annie-record-origin-findings-nov-2015]
- **The "day two/day three" framing is corrected.** The dossier baseline
  placed escalation on days two and three; the corpus puts the merger
  idiom ninety minutes after first in-person contact. Escalation speed
  is measurable in hours, which is the hard date any later claim about
  the relationship's tempo has to survive. [dat:0165]

## What this is not

- **Not a seduction.** The mutual-exit correction (Annie ending her own
  partner the same week; Suz brokering Dan's side with cocaine and a car
  offer) reframes the origin as a transaction negotiated in 72 hours, not
  a one-sided pursuit. [dat:0161]
- **Not Dan's line on June 1.** The June 1, 2026 exit line ("closure")
  is Dan's, not Annie's — the 2026-08-13 correction on the main Annie
  page; end-fight.md still carries the stale attribution
  (propagation failure, INGEST-NOTES).
