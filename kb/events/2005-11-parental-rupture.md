## What happened

In November 2005, when Dan was seventeen, his father Rick disclosed his
cocaine addiction and entered rehabilitation, and his mother Suzanne was
revealed to have been having an affair with the family's own cocaine
dealer during his treatment. The marriage ended. Dan names this event the
hinge of his life — the point the rest of the psychological record
organizes itself around.

The substance is not incidental to the rupture: cocaine is the thread that
connects both betrayals — the father's addiction and the mother's affair
with the man who supplied it — to a single source.

## Evidence for

- **Consistency across the canonical record.** The November 2005 dating is
  carried by the old wiki, the Developmental Origins dossier, MEMORY.md's
  life-hinge event, and the agent summaries. No source disputes the month.
  [dat:1027-developmental-origins-dossier-only] records the dating as
  "consistent with the canonical record."
- **Durability as self-narrative.** Dan has held this as the central trauma
  node for two decades, from early self-accounts through the 2026 forensic
  self-analysis. The Developmental Origins page (2026) re-encodes the
  same event as the axiom "intimacy is a precursor to abandonment, loyalty
  is conditional, emotional security is an illusion."

## Evidence against / limits

- **No contemporaneous record exists.** The held message corpus's earliest
  rows are years later; the wiki's own gap list states Suz's account of
  the 2005 rupture is "still entirely Dan-side" and "the contemporaneous
  record begins in 2015, ten years after."
- **Single-witness testimony.** Rick and Suz have no on-record account of
  the event anywhere in the corpus. The dealer's identity is not named in
  any held source.
- **The dating was not re-derived from primary records.** [dat:1027] is
  explicit: the Nov 2005 dating was checked against MEMORY.md for
  consistency, not re-derived from a dated primary record.

## Contradictions the ingest found

- **The pre-rupture exposure complicates the origin story.** Cocaine was a
  known, "adult" household behavior from 2001 — four years before the
  rupture — and Dan's first use dates to the ski-crew context of
  2004–2006, overlapping the rupture rather than following it. A clean
  "the divorce caused the drug use" reading does not survive the
  timeline: the substance was already inside the house and inside him.
  (corpus__3.txt §System genesis; [dat:1393-seven-springs-first-exposure])
- **Selective exclusion, not abstinence.** Four family members across three
  generations were functional alcoholics, and alcohol — not cocaine — is
  the substance present at the household's defining betrayals. Dan
  adopted cocaine freely and excluded alcohol. The old wiki reads this as
  abstinence coded to the *mechanism of betrayal*, not to substance use.
  (corpus__3.txt §Family context)
