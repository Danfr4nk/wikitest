Six advances against the Fran Coldren estate were issued to Dan between Oct 2019 and Jun 2020, totalling $42,500 — a sequence that retired one of the old wiki's 49 live contradictions (dat:0049), and did it by reading the source rather than adjudicating between two accounts.

**Timeline (amounts stated by the attorney or the executor in their own words, inbound)**
- 2019-10-12 — $5,000 from the attorney ("I will write a check payable to you for $5000"; re-issued 2019-10-16 to a different payee — one payment, not two).
- 2020-02-04 — $10,000 from the attorney ("I will prepare a check for $10,000").
- 2020-03-06 — $7,000 from the attorney ("I will prepare a check for $7000").
- 2020-03-27 — $3,000 from the executor ("I just sent rod a text authorizing $3,000").
- 2020-04-17 — $7,500, authorised by the executor and issued by the attorney in two separate messages the same day.
- 2020-06-23 — $10,000, confirmed retrospectively by the executor on 2020-08-26: "on June 23,2020 we issued you a check for $10,000 which I told you would be deducted from the final distribution."
- 2020-08-26 — the executor states the final share: $144,069.31 gross, $134,069.31 after the June advance, available 2020-09-01.

**The contradiction it dissolved:** wiki/people/rod-banks recorded five advances totalling $32,500 (none dated 2020-06-23), while the spine timeline carried a single line, "2020-06-23 · Estate advance issued · $10,000." The wiki's own hypotheses were that either there were two $10,000 advances four months apart or the spine was wrong. The estate threads (149 messages, 2017-04-19 to 2020-08-26, read in full; every numeric token enumerated to rule out a seventh) confirm the first hypothesis. Neither page was wrong — each saw part of the sequence and neither saw the whole.

**New figure:** the final share ($144,069.31 / $134,069.31) is first-party from the executor and was new to this knowledge base.

**Limit:** the threads end 2020-08-26. Whether the September distribution arrived as described is never_observed here — the message says Dan's mother was to receive and deposit the cheque, and nothing confirms it happened. A request confirmed is not a payment made.
