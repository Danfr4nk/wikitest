## What happened

Across **2026-08-16 23:37 → 2026-08-19 15:15**, the relationship ended —
**1,199 messages** (811 Dan / 388 inbound) in the event page's window
[`dat:0257`](../data/0257-morgantown-call-leverage-campaign.md). It opens
with a phone call Dan did not place and recorded anyway: **15 minutes 27
seconds** on his MacBook (`Morgantown St.m4a`, container 2026-08-17
03:54:49 UTC = 23:54:49 EDT) — the filename is location evidence, macOS
Voice Memos naming the street the device stands on: Morgantown St, Dan's
street, placing him at home on a call, not at the scene
[`dat:0257`](../data/0257-morgantown-call-leverage-campaign.md).

**What is on the recording** (verified against the operator-supplied
timestamped transcript filed 2026-08-20; 204 turns — Coles 99, Annie 55,
Dan 50): Coles confesses to searching her phone while she slept —
*"She was sleeping, I went through her phone. Yeah, and now what?"*
(00:43) — and holds it the whole call; Annie asks for her phone five
times, says she wants to leave from 04:16, says she is being held hostage
twice, alleges she was hit and threatened, and says the police were called
before over this. Coles interrogates her on tape to make her say Dan
prostituted her, conditions the phone on the answer, threatens her parents
to force it, and tells Dan to kill himself. Dan's contribution is almost
entirely one sentence — *"Give her her phone and let her leave"* — plus
an offer to drive (12:15). At 13:30, while the call is still running, Dan
aims the recording at Annie's mother — *"I'm gonna send this whole thing
to Ellen"* — so the artifact was **leverage from inception**, not
repurposed later
[`dat:0257`](../data/0257-morgantown-call-leverage-campaign.md).

**The corrections printed on the page:** at 04:43 Annie says *"Yes, save
it"* under interrogation — the page retracts its own earlier "she never
says yes," holding instead that she never *freely* affirmed the
allegation. The Jerel Coles people page still carries the defeated

[`dat:0257`](../data/0257-morgantown-call-leverage-campaign.md),
[`dat:0412`](../data/0412-jerel-coles-page-patch-state.md).

**The campaign, Aug 17–19:** the demand is never money or contact but a
**statement** — say in writing she is done with him. The named target is
**Ellen's work email**. Aug 17 12:52 Annie discloses a suicide attempt by
strangulation (no independent corroboration; the page does not treat it as
settled, and the campaign did not pause for it). The false send: Aug 18
19:35 Dan writes *"i did"*; at 19:38 he admits *"it wasn't actually
sent… I knew you would suddenly come back to life"* — after which Dan's
own assertions of execution can no longer serve as evidence of execution.
**It happened again:** Aug 18 21:46 Coles seizes her phone a second time,
then an **SOS sequence 22:18–23:41** (six SOSs; *"Betty. Milo. Whatever
sos words"* — the two dogs as a duress code). The asymmetry doubles
(3.1:1 → 6.3:1 text volume; Dan says goodbye 37 times and threatens to
block 15 times in 67 hours)
[`dat:0257`](../data/0257-morgantown-call-leverage-campaign.md).

**The catalyst:** Aug 19 06:33 Annie writes *"He didn't rape me"* in the
group chat — a public retraction of the accusation she kept making to Dan
privately, issued in front of Coles; the page does not adjudicate the
rape claim (asserted to Dan repeatedly, denied by Coles in writing,
denied by Annie in the group chat, re-asserted to Dan afterwards — all

*"Do NOT ever think that enough time has passed…"* — the vector every

[`dat:0257`](../data/0257-morgantown-call-leverage-campaign.md).
