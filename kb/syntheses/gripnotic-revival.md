## What it explains that the parts don't

The entity node counts the mentions; the datum verifies them. Neither
states what the pattern means, and the meaning is the synthesis:
**the DJ identity is held as self-concept and almost
unmentioned as activity** — corrected 2026-09-11 from "production

([`dat:music-djing-true-love-not-production`](../data/dat-music-djing-true-love-not-production.md));

tooling, zero sessions.
The alias clock explains why: renames do biographical work. A new alias
is not a new musical direction; it is a timestamp — a way of marking
that an era has begun. GRIPNOTIC's three rows on April 6, 2026 are the
timestamp of the current era's beginning, ten weeks before the June 1
closure ended the last one.

That reframes the "revival" honestly. It is real as signal — the name
enters the corpus, the links go out, the MOGZART revival a month
earlier verifies — and unobserved as work. The distinction matters
because the biography's failure mode is precisely the confusion of the
two: the ledger's inverted calibration band (stated certainty ≈ 0.25
actual) is a warning against promoting signals to facts. The revival is
a signal. The node keeps it one.

## Where it breaks and what it can't settle

**There is no held evidence of music being made.** The 'oof' set exists
as links shared to contacts; its contents are not in the record.
The March 2026 MOGZART revival is handle activity, not finished work.
The thirteen-year pattern — remixes and AI voice drops, never sung
originals — is page testimony, and the "Goodbye Demo" December 26, 2015
anchor and the 63–85% measurement are uncheckable in the held corpus
(0 hits for both), single-provenance from the context-core/alias pages'
own material ([`dat:1025`](../data/1025-alias-dates-held-checks.md)).

**The clock thesis is fitted to four aliases.** Four data points make a
suggestive pattern, not a law. A rename that did genuine musical work —
a real genre break under a new name — would show the thesis is
overfitted, and the falsifier is written to catch exactly that.

**The current status is unobserved.** GRIPNOTIC entered the corpus ten
weeks before the June 1, 2026 closure. Whether the name survived the
severance, whether the "current primary brand" designation still holds
in September 2026, whether anything was ever released under it — none
of it is in the held record past April 6. The revival is dated; its
continuation is not.
