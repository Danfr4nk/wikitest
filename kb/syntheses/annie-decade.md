## The argument

Eleven years with Annie Ulmer is not just the longest relationship in the
record. It is the only one in the record large enough to be measured — and
what measurement produced is the single most counterintuitive fact in the
corpus:

**In 129 distinct severance episodes across the 95,067-row Annie corpus,
every episode that had a following message resumed contact. 100%. Median
gap thirty-six seconds. All-time maximum 46 hours.**

A declared exit in this relationship is not an exit. It is a rung on the
escalation ladder — 36 seconds being what a check-in looks like when it
gets answered. That reading is not an interpretation imposed on the data;
it is what the data forced on 2026-08-23 when the old wiki's own
127/110 pair failed to reconcile with the dossiers' description of ~100%
re-engagement, and an independent recount (Dan-sent severance language,
hits within 24 hours collapsed to one episode) returned 129 against the
dossiers' 127 by a method the dossiers did not use
([`evt:2026-08-23-attachment-model-resolved`](../events/2026-08-23-attachment-model-resolved.md)).

The decade is the corpus's one fully instrumented attachment experiment:
97,768 unique messages, 2015-11-28 → 2026-06-05, the largest single
relationship record, counted to within 0.2% of the operator's own
correction (the prior wiki's 126,683 figure is ~29% overstated and may not
be quoted) ([`ent:annie-ulmer`](../entities/annie-ulmer.md)). Nothing else
in the biography has this sample size. Everything else is case study; this
is a dataset.

## The evidence it synthesizes

**The origin: the bond switch, corrected.** The corpus opens 2015-11-28
already mid-relationship — Dan calling her "the love of my life," Annie
answering "I've known that since day one" — and the escalation is
measurable in hours: "YOU ARE MY EVERYTHING" went out at 03:24 EST on
November 29, roughly ninety minutes after the first in-person meeting at
Uniontown Country Club. The November 29 meeting is a first in-person
meeting, not a first contact. Two corrections sit inside the origin story:
the 24-hour switch from Alexis was **Dan's** move, previously misattributed
to Annie (Ally's 2026-08-02 correction reversed the subject), and Alexis
made the introduction herself — "HAPPY ONE WEEK SINCE LEX HANDED YOU TO
ME" ([`evt:2015-11-annie-relationship-starts`](../events/2015-11-annie-relationship-starts.md),
[`evt:2015-11-alexis-evicted`](../events/2015-11-alexis-evicted.md)).

**The control case: Alexis Armel.** Six years (Thanksgiving 2009 → November
2015) that the wiki treats as the decade's control — the long relationship
that "occupied the attachment slot without ever activating it." The
comparison is load-bearing: same duration class as a serious relationship,
no measured exit-episode idiom, and the relationship that preceded the
decade to the day — the overlap is literal, the eviction and the bond
switch landing within the same 72-hour window
([`ent:alexis-armel`](../entities/alexis-armel.md)).

**The comparison case: Kristin Prentiss.** Ten weeks, August–November 2025,
20,014 held messages, ended over $40 — and the block imposed December 9,
2025 has been held since. She is the record's one clean severance: the
most exhaustively counted thread in the corpus, the terminal sixteen
minutes verifying message-for-message. Against a base rate of 100%
resumption, one ten-week relationship did what eleven years did 129 times
and never finished ([`evt:2025-08-kristin-starts`](../events/2025-08-kristin-starts.md),
[`evt:2025-11-kristin-ends`](../events/2025-11-kristin-ends.md)).

**The arrangement era.** An authored non-monogamous arrangement with paid
and unpaid third parties; the summer-2019 escort window under the alias
"Hazel" (old-wiki testimony, no held-corpus corroboration yet); the
November 2018 Alexis arrangement reunion confirmed verbatim (*"Initially I
offered her $3000 to be a plaything for Annie"*); per-year direction
counts with Annie out-messaging Dan in every held year except 2015 and
2025–26 ([`ent:annie-ulmer`](../entities/annie-ulmer.md)).

**The terminal phase.** The December 2025 spike (4,657 messages, replicating
within 1% in the held thread; Annie's engagement halving while Dan's
holds) ([`evt:2025-12-message-spike`](../events/2025-12-message-spike.md)).
The June 1, 2026 group-chat closure: an unbroken Dan outbound run
ending 00:27:49 EDT — *"Goodbye forever. This was not how it should have
ended but. sic semper lupanis."* — misattributed to Annie until 2026-08-13;
666 messages May 31–June 1 (601 Dan / 65 Annie); then the closure failed at
fifty-two days, "by a wide margin the most durable severance in a
relationship with 127 declared exits"
([`evt:2026-06-01-annie-closure`](../events/2026-06-01-annie-closure.md)).
The May–June Coles entanglement ([`evt:2026-05-jerel-coles-entanglement`](../events/2026-05-jerel-coles-entanglement.md))
and the August 16–19, 2026 terminal rupture: 1,199 messages across four
days, opening with a 15-minute-27-second call Dan recorded on his MacBook
without placing it, Coles confessing to searching Annie's phone while she
slept and holding it the whole call, Annie asking for her phone five
times and saying she wants to leave from 04:16
([`evt:2026-08-16-annie-terminal-end`](../events/2026-08-16-annie-terminal-end.md)).

**The verdict on the rupture.** The August grievance verdict (2026-08-20)
adjudicates ten stated complaints against dated evidence: seven fully
supported, two partly, one false — and the false one is the most useful.
Grievance 6 (*"You won't even say what you want"*) scores FALSE: the held
corpus shows Annie stating a want at 2026-08-17 20:12 EDT — *"I want to be
alone. I want to get help"* — repeated Aug 19 15:00/15:06. A binary
"him or me" demand could not register a third-option answer as an answer.
The structural finding is **capacity**: documented coercive control by a
third party plus a disclosed suicide attempt relocate culpability without
reducing the wrongs
([`evt:2026-08-19-august-grievance-verdict`](../events/2026-08-19-august-grievance-verdict.md)).

## What it explains that the parts don't

No single event node in the chain carries the finding that makes the
decade matter: **the exit idiom is load-bearing evidence about the
attachment model, and it runs the wrong direction.** Everything about how
these two fought — the block declarations, the goodbyes, the "never
contact me again" — reads as separation. Measured against the full corpus
it is the opposite: separation language is the relationship's check-in
protocol, deployed 129 times, answered within 36 seconds in the median
case. That is why the June 1 closure's 52-day survival is the most
durable severance "by a wide margin" — the distribution is not a normal
distribution with a long tail; it is a wall at 46 hours, and June 1 is
the only episode that ever crossed it.

It also explains why the current severance is designed the way it is.
The 120-day horizon test (December 19, 2026) is meaningful rather than
merely long precisely because the base rate is the strongest the
architecture has ever had: 129/128, 100%, 36-second median. A test
without a base rate is a wish. This one is an experiment — see
[`syn:severance-2026`](severance-2026.md).

And it explains the decade's asymmetry with the rest of the biography.
The Kristin block held. The Alexis thread decayed by the mention. The
Annie thread ran eleven years and 97,768 messages and never once
completed an exit it declared. Three relationships, three exit regimes —
and only one of them is measured at scale.

## Where it breaks and what it can't settle

**The record is not complete, and the incompleteness is structural.**
2020-11 → 2024-12 is empty — five messages in four years — and it is a
hole in the record, not a quiet period in the relationship
([`ent:annie-ulmer`](../entities/annie-ulmer.md)). Every claim about those
years is constrained to non-message evidence. If the hole ever fills, the
causes of the terminal rupture may move from August 2026 back into an
unobservable window, and no falsifier in this node can reach them first.

**The current ending is in progress.** As of this writing the block is
reported held (2026-09-09 record), but the August 26 "first held boundary"
claim fired its own falsifier within 48 hours (Aug 27–31 resumption at
16/39/108/84/65 messages/day, two-sided). The sequence is boundary
honored, violated, re-held — and "terminal" is a prediction, not an
observation.

**The arithmetic has an open seam.** The closure "failed within 52 days"
but the fireworks email that ended the severance is dated July 4 — 33
days after June 1, not 52. One of the two figures is off and the corpus's
own resolution stands unresolved ([`ent:annie-ulmer`](../entities/annie-ulmer.md)).

**Handle attribution is a standing caveat.** At least six inbound messages
from Annie's handle were typed by Coles. During Coles-present episodes the
speaker is not the handle, and no contact claim scores without sender
verification.
