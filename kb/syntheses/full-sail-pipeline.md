## The argument

Full Sail University is the one institution in Dan's biography that
worked — not as an experience (the record is thin on what it felt like)
but as a record. It is the only place in sixteen years where
contemporaneous, platform-timestamped evidence outlasts his own memory
of the event, and the only institution whose paperwork survived his
recollection of it.

The fact is load-bearing because the biography is otherwise built on
retrospective testimony. The November 2005 hinge has no contemporaneous
record. The estate's deep-cycle money rests on a re-read. The
day-zero Suboxone date is computed by a model from logs. Against that
background, Full Sail is the control: a September 2009 graduation —
top 5% of the class, Pro Tools HD certified — with four dated artefacts
that sat on the record for years with no reading that took all four,
until the subject read the published wiki on 2026-09-09, found the month
wrong, and supplied in one sentence the fact that reconciled them.

## The evidence it synthesizes

**The pipeline.** ~August 2008: arrives in Orlando; the day after
arrival, Danielle Onesi — the first girlfriend (~2005–2009), now Suz's
closest friend — picks out the cat Gabe
([`dat:0487`](../data/0487-danielle-onesi-first-girlfriend-gabe-james.md)).
2009-08-31, tweet: *"i start my last month of college tomorrow"* —
pointing at a September finish. **2009-09-26**: *"I acually just
graduated today for music production"* — said the same day to two
people, in two Facebook messages, read directly from the archive
([`dat:0045`](../data/0045-facebook-graduation-claim-september-2009.md)).
2009-11-30 → 2009-12-19: tweets — *"pro tools lab. noice"* (Nov 30),
*"aced my practical without headphones"* (Dec 14), *"in class...."*
(Dec 15), *"taking aws final with it"* (Dec 19). 2010-01-26: the Pro
Tools 210M certification, dated to the minute in platform-timestamped
tweets. 2010-02-17: Suboxone day-zero, at the tail of the Full Sail
window. 2010-02-28: leaves Florida for New York City
([`evt:2009-09-full-sail-graduation`](../events/2009-09-full-sail-graduation.md),
[`ent:full-sail`](../entities/full-sail.md),
[`evt:2010-02-17-suboxone-start`](../events/2010-02-17-suboxone-start.md)).

**The correction.** The old wiki carried "graduated August 2009" —
which matched none of the four artefacts. The September 26 messages said
September; the December tweets looked like continued enrollment. Dan's
2026-09-09 correction is not a fourth competing hypothesis; it is the
one state of the world in which all four artefacts are true at once: he
graduated in September, then stayed on through December auditing one
class, running labs, and finishing his Pro Tools operator certification.
A graduate can sit in a lab, take a practical, and sit an AWS final —
and would write a tweet about it exactly as if still enrolled
([`dat:0058`](../data/0058-graduation-september-2009-then-audit-and-certification.md),
[`evt:2026-09-09-graduation-correction-published`](../events/2026-09-09-graduation-correction-published.md)).

**Why the correction is a different kind of evidence.**
[`dat:0058`](../data/0058-graduation-september-2009-then-audit-and-certification.md)
treats testimony that *explains residue* as a different, stronger
instrument than testimony that merely asserts a fact. The September
claim did not just add a date; it dissolved a contradiction that four
artefacts had held open for years, on two pages of the prior wiki that
explicitly declined to resolve it (*"Neither is corrected on the
strength of the other"*). That is the signature of a true correction:
it makes previously incompatible evidence compatible without moving any
of it.

**The people inside the pipeline.** Alexis Armel enters here — the
Orlando/Full Sail orbit, first named publicly 2009-12-14, the
Thanksgiving-2009 trip that brought them together also the final active-use
episode before the 2010-02-17 stabilization
([`ent:alexis-armel`](../entities/alexis-armel.md)). The pipeline's end
is the opiate arc's end and the NYC era's beginning, all inside eleven
days: certification January 26, day-zero February 17, Amtrak February 18.
Full Sail is not just the education chapter. It is the hinge between the
dark era and everything after.

## What it explains that the parts don't

The Full Sail nodes, individually, are a graduation date and a
correction event. Together they demonstrate the one thing the
biography's epistemics most needs: **that contemporaneous records can
be wrong in the way the testimony ledger never scores.**
[`int:contemporaneous-is-not-the-same-as-true`](../interpretations/contemporaneous-is-not-the-same-as-true.md)
makes the general point — a class schedule, a docket entry and a
delivery receipt were made for something other than the reader's
benefit, and a message telling an old friend things are going great was
made for a reader. The September 26 "just graduated today" messages are
the concrete instance: a reconnection with someone he hadn't spoken to
in years, the graduation announced alongside a move to Los Angeles to
work in a studio — five months later he moved to New York instead.
The ledger's `slant` column separates errors that would flatter from
errors that would condemn, and applies it only to the testimony under
grading, never to the evidence doing the grading. Full Sail is where
that asymmetry has a name and a date.

It also explains why the pipeline metaphor is the right one. He entered
an institution, extracted the credential (degree September, Pro Tools
certification January), and exited into the next life phase within
weeks. That is the shape of every functional system in the record —
the Suboxone regimen is the other one — and it is the opposite of the
family system and the Annie decade, which are states, not pipelines.
Institutions that work are the ones he can leave.

## Where it breaks and what it can't settle

**The transcript was never produced.** The prior wiki held the
graduation disagreement open across two pages and named the artefact
that would settle it. Dan's correction reconciles all four artefacts,
which is strong — but reconciliation is still inference, and the one
document that would make it fact is not in any archive anyone holds.
This node inherits the condition: high confidence, not certainty.

**The September 26 messages may be performance.** The reconnection
context, the LA-studio claim alongside the graduation announcement,
the move to New York five months later instead — the ledger's slant
axis was built for exactly this question. The correction treats the
messages as true because they cohere with the tweet and the
certification; it does not rule out that a performed message can be
accurate. Both readings survive.

**"The one institution" is a claim about the rest of the biography.**
It holds against the current record — no other institution in sixteen
years produces this density of timestamped corroboration — but it is a
universal claim over an incomplete corpus, and the 2020–2024 hole is
exactly the kind of span where a second institution could be hiding.
The falsifier is written accordingly.
