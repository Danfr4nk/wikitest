## The argument

The Frank family operates as a **two-pole attachment machine**. One pole
never leaves: Suzanne Frank — extreme availability, the second-largest
correspondence in the corpus, cohabiting with Dan at 463 Morgantown St,
the give-and-invoice financial pattern running through the whole thread.
The other pole withdraws: Rick Frank — warm-but-strained, low-frequency
contact, a thirteen-message unanswered tail across 2025 after Dan himself
proposed a get-together and then went silent, the corpus's clearest
instance of a channel dying from Dan's side rather than Rick's.

Everything else in the biography is oscillation between those poles.
The 2005 rupture is the machine's origin story. Fran is the third point
that made it a triangle instead of a line. Diane is the proof that the
maternal line has a hostile setting. And the money — the estate, the
deep-cycle capital, the Saratoga sale — is the current that runs between
the poles, never settling.

## The evidence it synthesizes

**The hinge.** In November 2005, when Dan was seventeen, Rick disclosed
his cocaine addiction and entered rehabilitation, and Suzanne was
revealed to have been having an affair with the family's own cocaine
dealer during his treatment. The marriage ended. Dan names this event
the hinge of his life — the point the rest of the psychological record
organizes itself around. The substance is not incidental: cocaine is the
thread connecting both betrayals — the father's addiction and the
mother's affair with the man who supplied it — to a single source.
Consistency across the canonical record is total and so is the
evidentiary thinness: **no contemporaneous record exists**, the held
message corpus's earliest rows are years later, and Rick and Suz have no
on-record account anywhere in the corpus. Single-witness testimony, held
as the central trauma node for two decades
([`evt:2005-11-parental-rupture`](../events/2005-11-parental-rupture.md)).

**The poles.** Suz: b. September 15, 1962, licensed PA realtor, Chapter 13
filed October 2024, extreme availability — the inverse of Rick's
withdrawal. The annual volume table (Dan ahead 2015–2016, near-tie 2017,
Suz permanently ahead from 2018) is page-attributed testimony,
unverifiable from the held slice; what verifies is the shape, not the
numbers ([`ent:suzanne-frank`](../entities/suzanne-frank.md)).
Rick: the concealed alternate reality of 2005 (his rehab admission, her
affair with the family dealer) is the corpus's named template for Dan's
later attachment pattern. Real repair episodes through 2020 and 2023–24,
then the February 2025 silence — Dan proposing the get-together Rick
accepted, then a full year of Rick trying to schedule the dinner Dan
proposed: *"What's going on?"*, *"Is there some reason that I'm in
timeout?"* — the held corpus confirming the tail nearly verbatim. Then,
2026-09-01: Dan's first message to Rick's number since February 2025 —
*"Hey I just got my schedule for the week and I'm going to be at work
until 11 tomorrow night. Are you busy towards the end of the week?"* —
the first held-corpus sighting of Dan voluntarily re-opening a channel
he let die ([`ent:rick-frank`](../entities/rick-frank.md),
[`evt:2026-09-01-rick-silence-breaks`](../events/2026-09-01-rick-silence-breaks.md)).

**The third point.** Frances Coldren (née Jesse Frances Thomas),
1920-08-15 – 2018-04-04 — Dan's maternal great-grandmother, who raised
him alongside Suz, named by both Dan and Suz as the guiding force of
their lives, the biggest life influence in his record. Dan and Annie
were her paid caregivers in 2017–18; the Numark NS7 she gifted him is
one of his life anchors; her death is the corpus's most documented grief
event ([`ent:frances-coldren`](../entities/frances-coldren.md),
[`evt:2017-2018-fran-caregiving`](../events/2017-2018-fran-caregiving.md),
[`evt:2018-fran-death`](../events/2018-fran-death.md)).

**The hostile pole.** Rebecca Diane Van Voorhis ("Diane Moore"), b.
1939 — Fran's only child, Dan's maternal grandmother, the one family
member whose documented posture toward Dan is hostility rather than
distance. During the April 2018 death vigil she barred Dan from Fran's
house: one letter, served late not early — the eviction notice served
2018-03-29, the letter read, forwarded and lawyered on 2018-04-03, the
day before Fran died, closing with *"any violation of the rules will be
considered trespassing."* Dan's own read that night: *"i think dave
wrote it"* — Dave Moore, Diane's second husband, who had been speaking
on her behalf to the hospital's social workers. Where a 2026
recollection and a 2018 message disagree about 2018, the message
governs ([`ent:diane-moore`](../entities/diane-moore.md)).

**The distant pole.** Vanessa Frank — younger sister, steady,
low-conflict, minimal contact, ski school in Vail, shared left politics.
709 messages, 2015-12-01 → 2026-03-01; the extraction records her living
in NYC too, unreconciled. Virtually everything below the thread
dimensions is unheld-dump testimony at low confidence
([`ent:vanessa-frank`](../entities/vanessa-frank.md)). Lisa Frank —
Rick's partner since at least December 2015, a teacher, a decade of
presence written into the record only in August 2026
([`ent:lisa-frank`](../entities/lisa-frank.md)).

**The current.** The money between the poles: the estate's deep-cycle
capital running Dan-to-Suz (~$14,000 of a ~$20,000 drawdown, 2018),
ending with mother and son threatening each other with court, each
claiming to be the creditor
([`dat:1293`](../data/1293-estate-spine-direction-reversal-dan-to-suz.md)).
The August 17, 2026 Coles exchange typing the Morgantown address
unprompted and threatening Dan's mother — the threat message confirming
Dan lives at 463 and claiming cameras on the property. The family system
is not background to the 2026 rupture; it is one of its targets.

## What it explains that the parts don't

No single entity node states the machine reading, but the machine is
visible only at the synthesis layer: **the biography's attachment
behavior is the family system running at larger scale.** The Annie
decade's 129 declared exits with 100% resumption is the Suz pole —
never leaving, availability as the constant — played out in a romantic
register. The Rick pole — withdrawal, the channel dying from his side —
is the shape of every employment exit and of the February 2025 silence
toward Rick himself, Dan dealing to his father the exact hand his
father dealt him. The 2005 hinge is not just a trauma node; it is the
machine's schematic: one parent consumed by the substance, the other
betraying through the substance's supplier, the child left holding the
single thread that connects them.

It also explains why Fran mattered the way she did. In a two-pole
system, the third point is the only stable position — the person who
raised him alongside Suz but was neither the leaver nor the left. Her
death in April 2018 removed the triangle's third vertex, and the
2018–2020 window that follows (the deep-cycle money, the creditor duel
with Suz, the supply-network era) is the two-pole machine running
without its stabilizer. The Numark NS7 she gifted him is a life anchor
because it is the one object in the record that came from the stable
point.

## Where it breaks and what it can't settle

**The hinge is single-witness.** The most load-bearing event in the
family system — the thing the rest of the psychological record organizes
itself around — has no contemporaneous record, no second witness, and
no held source naming the dealer. The wiki's own gap list states Suz's
account of the 2005 rupture is "still entirely Dan-side." A synthesis
built on a single witness is a synthesis with one point of failure, and
this node names it rather than burying it.

**The poles are partly unheld.** The Suz volume table, the Rick repair
episodes, the 2015–2022 bulk of both threads: page-attributed testimony
from the fuller dump, neither verified nor falsifiable from the held
slice. The two-pole shape is high-confidence; the numbers inside it are
not.

**Diane's hostility has no visible cause.** The record documents the
exclusion letter in detail and the motive not at all. A family system
with an unexplained hostile pole is a system with a missing variable,
and this node does not invent one.

**2026-09-01 is one message.** The Rick re-opening is the first
held-corpus sighting of Dan voluntarily re-opening a channel he let die
— and it is a single scheduling message eight days old as of this
writing. Whether it is a repair or a blip is not yet observable.
